# Computer Labs - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/t/teaching-studios-computer-laboratories
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What **computer labs and teaching studios** are available to CityUHK students?

## Answer

## Teaching Studios

To facilitate specialised software teaching and computer-assisted teaching, the **Computing Services Centre (CSC)** provides **8 teaching studios** with sizes ranging from **69 to 112 PCs** in the **Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building (LI)** and **Yeung Kin Man Academic Building (YEUNG)**.

## Access Requirements

- Open to students and staff with a valid **Smart Card** and **LAN username and password**

## Facilities

- Equipped with high-quality **projection and audio-visual facilities** connected to the instructor's computer
- Personal computers run on **Microsoft Windows**
- All PCs are networked in the form of a **student intranet** with access to a list of software
- If required software is not on the list, instructors may request to have the software added via **CSC Work Request**

## Opening Hours

| Day | Teaching Studios |
|-----|------------------|
| Mon - Fri | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Sat | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Sun | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Public Holidays | 08:30 - 23:00 |

The CSC may extend the hours if there is a need. When there are no reservations, students are free to use the computers for their coursework.

## Related Services

- **Remote Desktop** - For staff to access their office PC remotely (requires MFA)
- **Virtual Desktop Service** - Virtual desktop access
- **Notebook Computer Loan** - Loan of notebook computers
