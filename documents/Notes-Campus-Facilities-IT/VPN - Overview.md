# VPN - Overview

## Question

What is the **Virtual Private Network (VPN)** at CityUHK and how do I use it?

## Answer

## What is the VPN?

The **Virtual Private Network (VPN)** allows current staff and students to securely connect to the campus network from outside the university and access campus resources as if they were on-campus.

## How to Use the VPN

- Off-campus remote computers or mobile devices must have the **VPN client software** installed
- Users will always be prompted to **authenticate** upon connection
- Once successfully authenticated, a secured and encrypted VPN connection is established to allow remote access to authorised resources on the campus network

## MFA Requirement

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is required whenever you access the **VPN** (Category B system). You have to log in with the MFA whenever you access the VPN.

## Related Services

- **Off-campus Wi-Fi** - Free Wi-Fi services outside the campus (Eduroam, CSL Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi.HK)
- **On-campus Wi-Fi** - CityUHK Wi-Fi network (CityU WLAN, Eduroam, Wi-Fi.HK via CityU)
