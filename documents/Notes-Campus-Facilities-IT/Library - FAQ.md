# Library - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/lib/about/contact.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about the **Run Run Shaw Library** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: Where is the Library located?

The **Run Run Shaw Library** is the university library of CityUHK. Its website is at https://www.cityu.edu.hk/lib/.

## Q2: How do I borrow library materials?

Use the **Circulation Counter** service. For enquiries, contact 3442-8316 or email lbasprs@cityu.edu.hk.

## Q3: How do I get help with research or referencing?

Use the **Virtual Instruction and Reference Help Desk** (3442-8395, lbinf@cityu.edu.hk) or the **Instruction and Reference Services** (3442-8395, lbinf@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q4: How do I request materials not held by the Library?

Use the **Interlibrary Loan Services** (3442-8824, lbilloan@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q5: How do I access course-specific reserved materials?

Use the **Course Reserve** service (3442-6039 / 3442-6068, lbres@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q6: How do I get IT help in the Library?

Use the **IT Help Desk** (3442-6963, libemt@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q7: How do I get help with CityU Scholars?

Contact the **CityU Scholars** support team (3442-6935, lbscholars@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q8: How do I get help with research data management?

Contact the **Research Data Management** team (3442-6935, lbrdms@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q9: How do I get help with Open Access?

Contact the **Open Access** team (3442-2207, lbopen@cityu.edu.hk).

## Q10: How do I get help with research impact measurement or author IDs?

Contact the **Research Impact Measurement** team (3442-6935, lbresearch@cityu.edu.hk) or the **Author IDs and Scopus Profiles Cleanup** team (3442-5744, lbresearch@cityu.edu.hk).
