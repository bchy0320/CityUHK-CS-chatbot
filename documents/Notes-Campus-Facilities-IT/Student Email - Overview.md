# Student Email - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/e/email
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What **email service** is provided to CityUHK students?

## Answer

## University Email Accounts

The University provides email services to all CityUHK staff, students and alumni. When they first join the university, they are automatically provided with a **Microsoft 365 email account** for conducting official university business.

**Microsoft 365 (M365)** is a suite of online productivity applications including email, calendar, contacts, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, etc.

## Email Accounts by User Type

| User Type | Email Domain | Plan |
|-----------|--------------|------|
| **Staff** | @cityu.edu.hk | Microsoft 365 A3 plan |
| **Students** | @my.cityu.edu.hk | Microsoft 365 A3 plan |
| **Alumni** | @my.cityu.edu.hk | Microsoft 365 A1 plan |
| **Retirees** (CityUHK Friends Email Service) | @friends.cityu.edu.hk | Google Workspace for Education |

## Optional Google Workspace Email

The University also offers **Google Workspace for Education** (with domain **@gapps.cityu.edu.hk**) as an additional and optional email service to staff and students for teaching/learning use.

- All **official correspondence** from the University will only be sent to the primary accounts described above
- Users may forward their email to the Google Workspace account at their own discretion
- A University Google Workspace account has the same features as a consumer Gmail account, with the additional benefits of **no advertisements** and **better privacy**
- The account will be removed when the account owner is no longer a CityUHK staff or student

## How to Access

- Log in with your **CityUHK EID**
- **MFA** is required when accessing Microsoft 365 through the CityUHK Single-Sign-On (SSO) system
