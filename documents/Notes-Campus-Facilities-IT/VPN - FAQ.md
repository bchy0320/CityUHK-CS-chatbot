# VPN - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/v/virtual-private-network-vpn
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about the **VPN** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: What is the VPN used for?

The **Virtual Private Network (VPN)** allows current staff and students to securely connect to the campus network from outside the university and access campus resources as if they were on-campus.

## Q2: Who can use the VPN?

Current **staff and students** can use the VPN service.

## Q3: What do I need to use the VPN?

Off-campus remote computers or mobile devices must have the **VPN client software** installed. Users will always be prompted to **authenticate** upon connection.

## Q4: Is the VPN connection secure?

Yes. Once successfully authenticated, a **secured and encrypted VPN connection** is established to allow remote access to authorised resources on the campus network.

## Q5: Do I need MFA for the VPN?

Yes. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is required whenever you access the VPN (a Category B system). You have to log in with the MFA whenever you access the VPN.

## Q6: What if I have VPN connection problems?

The **IT Service Desk** can help troubleshoot Wi-Fi and VPN connection problems. You may visit the Service Desk in person or call for help.
