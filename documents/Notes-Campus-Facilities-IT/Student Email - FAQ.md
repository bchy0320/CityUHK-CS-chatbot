# Student Email - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/e/email
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about **student email** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: What email account do I get as a student?

Students are automatically provided with a **Microsoft 365 email account** (with domain **@my.cityu.edu.hk**) hosted on Microsoft 365 with an **A3 plan**.

## Q2: What is included in Microsoft 365?

**Microsoft 365 (M365)** is a suite of online productivity applications including email, calendar, contacts, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, etc.

## Q3: Is there an optional Google email account?

Yes. The University offers **Google Workspace for Education** (with domain **@gapps.cityu.edu.hk**) as an additional and optional email service for teaching/learning use. It has the same features as a consumer Gmail account, with **no advertisements** and **better privacy**.

## Q4: Where will official correspondence be sent?

All **official correspondence** from the University will only be sent to the primary accounts (e.g. @my.cityu.edu.hk). Users may forward their email to the Google Workspace account at their own discretion.

## Q5: What happens to my Google Workspace account when I leave?

The account will be **removed** when the account owner is no longer a CityUHK staff or student.

## Q6: What email do alumni use?

Alumni use the same accounts they used when they were students (with domain **@my.cityu.edu.hk**). The subscription plan is **A1** instead of A3.

## Q7: Do I need MFA to access my email?

Yes. **MFA** is required when accessing Microsoft 365 through the CityUHK Single-Sign-On (SSO) system.
