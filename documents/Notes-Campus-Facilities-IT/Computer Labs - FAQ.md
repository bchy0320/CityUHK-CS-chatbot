# Computer Labs - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/t/teaching-studios-computer-laboratories
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about **computer labs and teaching studios** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: How many teaching studios are there?

The **Computing Services Centre (CSC)** provides **8 teaching studios** with sizes ranging from **69 to 112 PCs**.

## Q2: Where are the teaching studios located?

They are located in the **Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building (LI)** and **Yeung Kin Man Academic Building (YEUNG)**.

## Q3: Who can use the teaching studios?

They are open to **students and staff** with a valid **Smart Card** and **LAN username and password**.

## Q4: What operating system do the PCs run?

The personal computers run on **Microsoft Windows**.

## Q5: What software is available?

All PCs are networked in the form of a **student intranet** with access to a list of software. If required software is not on the list, instructors may request to have the software added via **CSC Work Request**.

## Q6: What are the opening hours?

| Day | Teaching Studios |
|-----|------------------|
| Mon - Fri | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Sat | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Sun | 08:30 - 23:00 |
| Public Holidays | 08:30 - 23:00 |

The CSC may extend the hours if there is a need.

## Q7: Can I use the computers when there are no classes?

Yes. When there are **no reservations**, students are free to use the computers for their coursework.

## Q8: How do I access my office PC remotely?

**Remote Desktop** gives staff full control over their office PC. It must be connected via the **Remote Desktop Gateway (RDGW)**, and users are required to enable **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** first.
