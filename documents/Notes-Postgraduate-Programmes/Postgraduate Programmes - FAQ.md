# Postgraduate Programmes - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/msc-computer-science/faq
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the postgraduate programmes at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## Can non-local students study part-time?

The study mode of non-local students is subject to the conditions and requirements set by the Hong Kong Immigration Department when their study visa/entry permit is granted. Currently, the vast majority of study visas/entry permits granted are based on the condition of full-time study, which requires the student to take 12-18 credit units per semester. They must maintain the required credit load for their approved study mode throughout the programme unless or until they obtain prior approval from the University.

Many students take 15 credit units in each semester so that they can graduate within one year. Some students take the course CS6520 Project (6 credit units) which spans through Semester B to the Summer Term.

## Will the University provide housing?

Students enrolled in a self-financed programme, including MSc programmes, are not eligible for University hostels that are subsidized by the Hong Kong Government. Off-campus affordable housing can be found in various residential neighborhoods close to the University, which is conveniently located right next to a major transportation hub. Visit the Student Residence Office's website for assistance and more information on searching for off-campus accommodation.

## What are my prospects of getting a job in Hong Kong after graduation?

The MSc programmes strike a careful balance between theory and practice. They significantly enhance the skills of their graduates. In fact, almost all past students who chose to stay in Hong Kong after graduation either found local employment opportunities or progressed for further research studies.

## When is the application period?

The application period for each intake usually begins in September until the published deadline and the course commencement is August/September. There is only one intake cohort each year.

## What application materials are required?

Applicants are required to provide a scanned copy of the following official documents:

1. Official enrolment certificate (for current students)
2. Official certificate in terms of Overall Average Mark issued by the university
3. Official academic transcript issued by university registry office, including overall average mark/CGPA, with explanation on grading system
4. English Proficiency Requirements (EPR) proof: IELTS (≥6.0) / CET6 (≥450) / TOEFL (≥79 for test taken prior to 21 January 2026 or 4 for test taken from 21 January 2026 onwards)
5. Degree Award Certificate and Graduation Certificate (for graduates)
6. Online Verification Report of Higher Education Degree Certificate (for Chinese Mainland graduates)
7. Personal statement and CV are required

> [!note] English Test Exemptions
> If your qualification is obtained in Australia/Canada/India/UK/USA, etc. and the medium of instruction is English, then the EPR is waived. These English tests are NOT acceptable: TOEFL iBT home edition, IELTS indicator, Duolingo, GMAT and GRE.

## How are applications assessed?

Assessment is done holistically on all submitted information. No "entrance score" can be provided. Interview will be arranged on a case-by-case basis. Applicants are expected to have university-level knowledge and skillsets in computer programming. Some prior knowledge about computer network and web application development is preferable.

## When are admission offers made?

Admission offers are mostly made in the months of November to June. The selection process is normally completed by the end of May for non-local applicants, or by the end of June for local applicants.
