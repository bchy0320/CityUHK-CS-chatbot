# MSc in Computer Science - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/msc-computer-science/admissions
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What is the **Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS)** programme at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Programme Overview

- **Programme Code**: P53 MSc Computer Science 理學碩士(計算機科學)
- **Mode**: Combined mode (full-time or part-time)
- **Funding**: Non-government Funded (self-financed)
- **Duration**: 1 year (full-time) or 2 years (part-time)

## Programme Aims

The programme aims to:

- Enable computer professionals to strengthen and upgrade their technical capabilities in computer software development
- Broaden students' knowledge and deepen their understanding of key issues of specific areas in computer science, including artificial intelligence, data science, information security, multimedia, and other related contemporary technologies
- Prepare graduates to take up research and advanced innovative development work in the industry or pursue higher research studies

## Programme Structure

To obtain the award, students must complete:

- **9 credit units** of core courses
- **21 credit units** of electives (including at least 3 credit units in Group I)

Total: **30 credit units**

Students may concentrate on a stream (AI, Data Science, or Information Security) or take electives without concentration.

## Study Modes

- **Full-time**: 12-18 credit units per semester, normally completed in 1 year
- **Part-time**: up to 11 credit units per semester, normally completed in 2 years
- **Maximum period of study**: 2.5 years (full-time) or 5 years (part-time/combined mode)

> [!note] Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science (PGDCS)
> The PGDCS is an intermediate award which a student may obtain after the completion of 24 credit units (including all core courses and at least 3 credit units of elective courses in Group I) with satisfactory results if the student decides not to continue with the MSCS programme.

## Entrance Requirements

- A recognised bachelor's degree in a computing discipline (Computer Science, IT, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent)
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree in a related discipline (Electronic Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Manufacturing Engineering, Quantitative Analysis, etc.) with applicable IT working experience

## English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants whose entrance qualification is from a non-English medium institution must fulfil:

- TOEFL: 79 (before 21 Jan 2026) or 4 (from 21 Jan 2026 onwards)
- IELTS: overall band score 6.0
- CET-6: 450

> [!note] Interview Requirement
> Applicants with an IELTS overall band score of 6.0 or a TOEFL overall score of 4 will be required to pass an interview for English proficiency. Applicants with a comparable overall score of 79 or above shown on the TOEFL report may be exempt from the interview requirement.

## Employment Prospects

The MSCS programme strikes a careful balance between theory and practice. Almost all past MSCS students who chose to stay in Hong Kong after graduation either found local employment opportunities or progressed for further research studies.
