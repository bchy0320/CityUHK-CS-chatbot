# MSc in Cybersecurity - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/msc-cybersecurity/admissions
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What is the **Master of Science in Cybersecurity (MSEC)** programme at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Programme Overview

- **Programme Code**: P91 MSc Cybersecurity 理學碩士(網絡安全)
- **Mode**: Combined mode (full-time or part-time)
- **Funding**: Non-government Funded (self-financed)
- **Duration**: 1 year (full-time) or 2 years (part-time)

## Programme Aims

The programme aims to:

- Enable computer professionals to strengthen and upgrade their technical capabilities in cybersecurity
- Enhance students' comprehensive knowledge and profound comprehension of specialised cybersecurity domains, encompassing both technical and practical aspects
- Prepare graduates to take up research and advanced innovative development work in the industry or pursue higher research studies in cybersecurity

## Programme Structure

To obtain the award, students must complete:

- **10 credit units** of core courses
- **21 credit units** of electives (at least 12 credit units in Group I, at most 9 credit units in Group II)

## Entrance Requirements

- A recognised bachelor's degree in a computing discipline (Computer Studies, IT, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent)
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree in a related discipline (Electronic Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Manufacturing Engineering, Quantitative Analysis, etc.) with applicable IT working experience
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree with substantial knowledge or background in information technology

## English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants whose entrance qualification is from a non-English medium institution must fulfil:

- TOEFL: 79 (before 21 Jan 2026) or 4 (from 21 Jan 2026 onwards)
- IELTS: overall band score 6.0
- CET-6: 450

> [!note] Interview Requirement
> Applicants with an IELTS overall band score of 6.0 or a TOEFL overall score of 4 will be required to pass an interview for English proficiency. Applicants with a comparable overall score of 79 or above shown on the TOEFL report may be exempt from the interview requirement.

## Employment Prospects

The MSEC programme strikes a careful balance between theory and practice. Almost all past students who chose to stay in Hong Kong after graduation either found local employment opportunities or progressed for further research studies.
