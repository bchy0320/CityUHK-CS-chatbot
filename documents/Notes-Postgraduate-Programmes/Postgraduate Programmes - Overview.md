# Postgraduate Programmes - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/programmes-overview

## Question

What **postgraduate programmes** are offered by the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Postgraduate Programmes

The Department of Computer Science offers the following postgraduate programmes:

| Programme | Mode | Source of Funding |
|-----------|------|-------------------|
| Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence | Combined mode | Non-government Funded |
| Master of Science in Computer Science | Combined mode | Non-government Funded |
| Master of Science in Cybersecurity | Combined mode | Non-government Funded |
| Master of Science in Electronic Commerce | Combined mode | Non-government Funded |
| Master of Philosophy / Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Science | Full time / Part time | Government Funded / Non-government Funded |

## Programme Codes

| Programme | Programme Code |
|-----------|---------------|
| MSc in Computer Science | P53 |
| MSc in Artificial Intelligence | P75 |
| MSc in Cybersecurity | P91 |
| MSc in Electronic Commerce | - |
| MPhil / PhD in Computer Science | - |

## Key Facts

- **MSc programmes** are taught postgraduate programmes (combined mode, i.e., full-time or part-time)
- **MPhil / PhD** are research degree programmes (full-time or part-time)
- All MSc programmes are **non-government funded** (self-financed)
- MPhil / PhD can be **government funded** or **non-government funded**
