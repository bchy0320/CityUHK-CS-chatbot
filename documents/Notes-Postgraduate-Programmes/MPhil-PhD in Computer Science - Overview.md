# MPhil/PhD in Computer Science - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/master-philosophy-doctor-philosophy/admissions
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **Master of Philosophy (MPhil) and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)** research degree programmes in Computer Science at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Programme Overview

- **Programme**: Master of Philosophy (MPhil) / Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Computer Science
- **Mode**: Full time / Part time
- **Funding**: Government Funded / Non-government Funded
- **Duration**: 
  - MPhil: 2 years (full-time) / 4 years (part-time)
  - PhD: 3 years (full-time) / 6 years (part-time)

## Programme Aims

The research degree programmes aim at equipping students with the skills and knowledge needed to meet the challenge of satisfying the present and future IT development needs.

- **MPhil**: Produces technical leaders in industrial research or development organisations. Focuses on strengthening students' knowledge and skills in certain areas of computer science.
- **PhD**: Develops skills in identifying research issues related to fundamental problems in computer science; formulating an original research plan; and creating an effective solution which leads to new knowledge in the field.

## Programme Structure

### MPhil (7 credit units)

- 2 credit units of research methodology courses
- 5 credit units of elective courses
- Thesis required

### PhD (14 credit units)

- 9 credit units of core courses
- 2 credit units of research methodology courses
- 3 credit units of elective courses
- Thesis required

## Entrance Requirements

### MPhil

- A recognised bachelor's degree with first or second class honours

### PhD

- A higher degree by research (e.g., MPhil)
- OR currently enrolled in an MPhil programme and seeking transfer to PhD
- OR a taught master's degree
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree with first class honours

## English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants from an institution where the language of teaching is not English should satisfy the minimum English proficiency requirements specified by both the University and individual Colleges and Schools. Please refer to the Research Degree Programmes website for details.

## Tuition Fees

| Type of Fee | Full time study | Remarks |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| Government-funded students: Tuition fee | HK$3,508 per month | Non-refundable; applicable to students within their stipulated study period |
| Government-funded students: Continuation fee | HK$877 per month | Non-refundable; applicable to students who have been approved for an extension of their study period |
| Self-financing students: Tuition fee | HK$7,016 per month | Non-refundable; applicable to students within their stipulated study period |
| Self-financing students: Continuation fee | HK$1,754 per month | Non-refundable; applicable to students who have been approved for an extension of their study period |

> [!note] Fees are subject to revision from time to time without prior notification, and any adjustments to fees will apply to both new and current students.

## Employment Prospects

Graduates of the research degree programmes are well-prepared for careers in industrial research and development organisations, or for pursuing further academic research.
