# MSc in Artificial Intelligence - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/academic-programmes/msc-artificial-intelligence/admissions
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What is the **Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence (MSAI)** programme at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Programme Overview

- **Programme Code**: P75 MSc Artificial Intelligence 理學碩士(人工智能)
- **Mode**: Combined mode (full-time or part-time)
- **Funding**: Non-government Funded (self-financed)
- **Duration**: 1 year (full-time) or 2 years (part-time)

## Programme Aims

The programme aims to:

- Enable computer professionals to strengthen and upgrade their technical capabilities in Artificial Intelligence
- Broaden students' knowledge and deepen their understanding of specific areas in Artificial Intelligence from both the technical and practical perspectives
- Prepare graduates to take up research and advanced innovative development work in the industry or pursue higher research studies in Artificial Intelligence

## Programme Structure

To obtain the award, students must complete:

- **10 credit units** of core courses
- **At least 21 credit units** of electives

Students may concentrate on a stream (Autonomous Driving, Generative AI, or Trustworthy AI) or take electives without concentration.

## Streams

- **Autonomous Driving (AD) Stream**
- **Generative AI (GAI) Stream**
- **Trustworthy AI (TAI) Stream**

## Entrance Requirements

- A recognised bachelor's degree in a computing discipline (Computer Studies, IT, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent)
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree in a related discipline (Electronic Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Manufacturing Engineering, Quantitative Analysis, etc.) with applicable IT working experience
- OR a recognised bachelor's degree with substantial knowledge or background in information technology

## English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants whose entrance qualification is from a non-English medium institution must fulfil:

- TOEFL: 79 (before 21 Jan 2026) or 4 (from 21 Jan 2026 onwards)
- IELTS: overall band score 6.0
- CET-6: 450

> [!note] Interview Requirement
> Applicants with an IELTS overall band score of 6.0 or a TOEFL overall score of 4 will be required to pass an interview for English proficiency. Applicants with a comparable overall score of 79 or above shown on the TOEFL report may be exempt from the interview requirement.

## Employment Prospects

The MSAI programme strikes a careful balance between theory and practice. Almost all past students who chose to stay in Hong Kong after graduation either found local employment opportunities or progressed for further research studies.
