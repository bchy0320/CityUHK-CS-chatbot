# Student Mentor Scheme - Overview

## Question

What **mentoring schemes** are offered by the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Mentoring Schemes

The Department of Computer Science offers **two** mentoring schemes to support students:

| Scheme | Target Audience | Purpose |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **Student Mentoring Scheme** | New students (freshmen) | Help new students adapt smoothly to university life |
| **Professional Mentorship Programme** | All students (mainly senior students) | Connect students with senior IT professionals for career guidance |

## Student Mentoring Scheme

- Helps new students adapt smoothly to university life
- Shapes senior students as "big brothers and sisters" for freshmen
- Regular informal gatherings organized by student mentors throughout the academic year
- Activities include: orientation tours, company visits, and study workshops

## Professional Mentorship Programme

- Provides a platform for students to interact with senior IT professionals in Hong Kong
- Gain exposure to different areas in IT (software development, support, networking, project management, etc.)
- Obtain guidance to shape students' career paths
- Develop communication and soft skills
- Duration: 10 months (e.g., October 2025 - July 2026)
