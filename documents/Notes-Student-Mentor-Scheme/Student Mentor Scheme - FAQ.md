# Student Mentor Scheme - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/student-life/student-mentoring/student-mentoring-scheme
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the Student Mentor Scheme at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## What is the difference between the Student Mentoring Scheme and the Professional Mentorship Programme?

- **Student Mentoring Scheme**: Focuses on helping **new students (freshmen)** adapt to university life. Senior students act as mentors ("big brothers and sisters").
- **Professional Mentorship Programme**: Focuses on **career development** by connecting students with **senior IT professionals** from industry. Runs for 10 months.

## Who can join the Student Mentoring Scheme?

- **Mentees**: New students (freshmen) joining the Department of Computer Science
- **Mentors**: Senior students who volunteer to guide freshmen

## Who can join the Professional Mentorship Programme?

- Students of the Department of Computer Science (mainly senior students and MSc students)
- Mentors are senior IT professionals from leading organisations in Hong Kong

## What activities are involved in the Student Mentoring Scheme?

- Orientation tours
- Company visits
- Study workshops
- Regular informal gatherings organized by student mentors

## What activities are involved in the Professional Mentorship Programme?

- Regular meetings between mentors and mentees
- Touring around mentors' companies
- Sharing sessions and happy hours
- Attending professional activities

## How long does the Professional Mentorship Programme last?

The programme operates for a duration of **10 months**. For example, the 2025-2026 cohort ran from October 2025 to July 2026.

## What companies do the mentors come from?

Mentors come from leading organisations in Hong Kong, including:

- Microsoft
- Meta
- PwC (PricewaterhouseCoopers)
- HKBN Enterprise Solutions
- PCCW
- TCL
- SmarTone
- Siemens
- Biel Crystal
- AI Guided
- China Mobile
- Checkpoint
- Manulife

## Are the mentors alumni of CityUHK CS?

Some mentors are alumni of the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong. For example, in the 2025-2026 cohort, Mr Alan HO (Meta) and Ir Dr John HUI (VTC) are alumni.

## How do I benefit from the Professional Mentorship Programme?

- Obtain guidance for future career development
- Understand the IT working environment
- Extend your social network
- Develop communication and soft skills
