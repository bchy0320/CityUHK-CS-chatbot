# International Exposure - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/geo/global-opportunities
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What **international exposure** opportunities are available to CityUHK CS students?

## Answer

## Overview

CityUHK offers a wide range of international exposure opportunities for students, including:

## 1. Student Exchange

- **Institutional-level**: 200+ exchange agreements via GEO
- **College-level**: College of Computing partners
- **Departmental-level**: IT University of Copenhagen, EPITA, University of Alicante
- **Duration**: Semester-long
- **Credits**: Transferable back to CityUHK

## 2. Study Tours

- **Short-term** (days to weeks)
- **Credit or non-credit bearing**
- **Professional business environments** and **cultural awareness**
- Organized by various colleges and departments

## 3. Summer Programmes

- Offered at partner institutions worldwide
- Typically during summer break

## 4. CityUHK Discovery@World Programme

- Tailored curriculums to individual locations
- Offers unique learning experiences

## 5. Semester at Sea

- Study aboard a ship while traveling around the world
- Unique international learning experience

## 6. Joint Bachelor's Degree Program

- With **Columbia University**
- Offers a joint degree opportunity

## 7. Global Experience for ALL Funding Scheme

- Financial support for international experiences
- Available to all students

## Why International Exposure Matters

- **Cultural awareness**: Gain understanding of different cultures
- **Professional skills**: Develop leadership, communication, and presentation skills
- **Academic growth**: Learn from different academic traditions
- **Career development**: Enhance employability and global perspective
- **Personal growth**: Build confidence and independence

## How to Get Started

1. Check the **GEO website** for global opportunities
2. Consult your **departmental exchange coordinator**
3. Check the **CS Student Exchange Canvas page** for useful information
4. Plan ahead and apply early
