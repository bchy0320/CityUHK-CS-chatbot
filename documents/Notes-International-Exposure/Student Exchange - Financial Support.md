# Student Exchange - Financial Support

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/student-life/student-exchange/student-exchange-outbound
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What **financial support** is available for CityUHK CS students going on exchange?

## Answer

## Student Exchange Fund

The **Student Exchange Fund** provides financial support to CityUHK full-time students in government-funded bachelor's degree programmes who are **Hong Kong permanent residents**.

## Special Fund for Non-local Outbound Exchange Students (SF)

The **Special Fund for Non-local Outbound Exchange Students (SF)** patronises CityUHK non-local students who study in full-time, government-funded bachelor's degree programmes.

## Global Experience for ALL Funding Scheme

The **Global Experience for ALL Funding Scheme** creates global learning opportunities for CityUHK students. This is another funding option available through the GEO.

## Key Points

- **Student Exchange Fund**: For Hong Kong permanent residents in government-funded bachelor's degree programmes
- **Special Fund (SF)**: For non-local students in full-time, government-funded bachelor's degree programmes
- **Global Experience for ALL**: Available through GEO for global learning opportunities
