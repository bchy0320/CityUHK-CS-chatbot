# Student Exchange - Application Process

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/geo/global-opportunities/student-exchange/outbound-student-exchange/application
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

How do I apply for the **Student Exchange Programme** at CityUHK? What are the selection criteria and requirements?

## Answer

## Application Rounds

Normally, there are **two rounds of applications** each academic year. Students are advised to consult the **Departmental Exchange Coordinators** before submitting applications through **AIMS** (under "Student Services").

## Selection Criteria

Exchange students are selected on **academic merit** and **overall suitability**. The weighting of the assessment criteria (subject to change) is as follows:

| Criteria | Weighting (%) |
|----------|--------------|
| CGPA | 50% |
| English language proficiency | 50% |
| Application statement | N/A (used when necessary) |

> [!note] English Language
> Students who can provide a valid **IELTS or TOEFL** result may have advantages in the selection process.

> [!note] Application Statement
> The application statement submitted as part of the application may be used for assessment when necessary.

## Offer Acceptance

Selected students will receive an exchange offer from CityUHK. The offer is **conditional**, meaning:

1. CityUHK will nominate the student to the host institution, but it does **not guarantee acceptance** by the host institution.
2. The student will receive further instructions from CityUHK to submit another application directly to the host institution.
3. **The student is responsible** for completing all application procedures and fulfilling all requirements of the host institution.
4. The official confirmation of the exchange is subject to acceptance by the host institution and final approval of CityUHK.

## Caution Money

Students are required to settle **caution money of HKD 1,000** when accepting the exchange offer from the University.

- Refund of caution money will be arranged when a student has completed the exchange programme to the satisfaction of the University and has submitted all required documents.
- Should a student fail to complete the exchange programme in full satisfaction or withdraw from programme after accepting the offer, the caution money will be **forfeited**.
- Caution money may be refunded within one year from the beginning of the exchange term, except for year-long exchange students, who will be given four more months for the claim.

## Important Notes

- No new offer will be issued to students who have already accepted an offer for the same exchange period in the previous round.
- Students must meet the **Minimum Requirements for Application for Student Exchange Programme** stipulated by the University.
