# Study Tour - Application Process

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/geo/global-opportunities
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

How do I apply for a **study tour** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Application Process

Study tours are typically organized by individual colleges or departments. The application process may vary, but generally follows these steps:

## Step 1: Find Study Tour Opportunities

- Check the **GEO website** for global opportunities
- Check your **college/department website** for specific study tour announcements
- Look for announcements via **student email** and **Canvas**

## Step 2: Check Eligibility

- Study tours are typically open to **full-time undergraduate students**
- Some study tours may have specific requirements (e.g., year of study, GPA)
- Check with the organizing unit for specific eligibility criteria

## Step 3: Submit Application

- Applications are typically submitted **online** via the organizing unit's platform
- Some study tours may require a **written application** or **interview**
- Deadlines vary by study tour

## Step 4: Attend Pre-departure Briefing

- Attend any required **pre-departure briefing** or orientation
- Complete any required **pre-departure preparation** (e.g., visa, insurance)

## Step 5: Participate in Study Tour

- Participate in the study tour activities
- Complete any required **assignments** or **reflections**

## Step 6: Post-tour Requirements

- Submit any required **post-tour reports** or **reflections**
- Complete any required **credit transfer** applications (if applicable)

## Key Tips

- **Apply early** - Study tours may have limited spots
- **Check deadlines** - Deadlines vary by study tour
- **Contact the organizing unit** - For specific questions about a study tour
- **Check funding** - Some study tours may have funding support available
