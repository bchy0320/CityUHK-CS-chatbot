# Student Exchange - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/geo/global-opportunities
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about **student exchange** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: When can I go on exchange?

Students can typically go on exchange during their **second year or later** of study. There are normally **two rounds of applications** each academic year. Students should plan ahead and consult with their departmental exchange coordinators.

## Q2: What are the eligibility requirements?

- **Institutional-level**: All full-time undergraduate students from compatible disciplines
- **College-level**: Full-time undergraduate students in College of Computing programmes
- **Departmental-level**: Full-time undergraduate CS students (specific partner universities)

## Q3: How are exchange students selected?

Selection is based on:
- **CGPA (50%)**
- **English proficiency (50%)**
- Application statement (N/A)

## Q4: Do I need to pay tuition at the host university?

No. Students pay **CityUHK tuition** during exchange. However, students may need to pay for:
- **Caution money** (HKD 1,000)
- **Accommodation** at the host university
- **Travel and living expenses**

## Q5: Can I transfer credits from exchange?

Yes, credits earned during exchange can be transferred back to CityUHK. However:
- Only transfer courses with at least **Grade C** or equivalent will be considered
- For some countries (e.g. Europe) using ECTS, **Grade D** (not the lowest passing grade) will be considered
- Students must submit the approved **Credit Transfer Pre-approved Application**

## Q6: What are the post-exchange requirements?

After returning from exchange, students must:
1. Complete an **online survey**
2. Submit a **500-word journal**
3. Submit **4 photos**
4. Submit **transcript** from host university
5. Apply for **credit transfer**
6. Give a **presentation** about their exchange experience

## Q7: What financial support is available?

- **Student Exchange Fund** - For HK permanent residents
- **Special Fund for Non-local** - For non-local students
- **Global Experience for ALL Funding Scheme** - For all students

## Q8: What partner universities are available?

CityUHK has exchange agreements with universities worldwide, including:
- **Institutional-level**: 200+ agreements via GEO
- **College-level**: College of Computing partners
- **Departmental-level**: IT University of Copenhagen, EPITA, University of Alicante

## Q9: How do I apply for exchange?

1. Check the **GEO website** for exchange opportunities
2. Consult your **departmental exchange coordinator**
3. Check the **CS Student Exchange Canvas page** for useful information
4. Submit your application during the application period

## Q10: What is the difference between exchange and study tour?

- **Exchange**: Semester-long, study at a partner university, credits transferable
- **Study Tour**: Short-term (days to weeks), professional/business exposure, credit or non-credit bearing
