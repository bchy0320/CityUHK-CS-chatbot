# International Exposure - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/geo/global-opportunities
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the common questions about **international exposure** opportunities at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: Does CityUHK have fixed annual study tours?

CityUHK offers various study tours organized by different colleges and departments throughout the year. These are typically organized during **summer breaks** and other academic breaks. The specific study tours offered may vary each year depending on the organizing unit. Students should check with their respective college/department for the latest study tour opportunities.

## Q2: How do I apply for a study tour?

Study tours are typically organized by individual colleges or departments. Students should:

1. Check the **GEO website** for global opportunities
2. Check their **college/department website** for specific study tour announcements
3. Contact the **Global Engagement Office (GEO)** for more information
4. Look for announcements via **student email** and **Canvas**

## Q3: Can I transfer credits from exchange back to CityUHK?

Yes, credits earned during exchange can be transferred back to CityUHK. However:

- Only transfer courses with at least **Grade C** or equivalent will be considered for credit transfer
- For some countries (e.g. Europe) using ECTS, **Grade D** (not the lowest passing grade) will be considered
- Students must submit the approved **Credit Transfer Pre-approved Application** (Non-GE Courses) / GE Courses Credit Transfer Pre-Approval Application

## Q4: What is the difference between exchange and study tour?

- **Exchange**: Semester-long, study at a partner university, credits transferable
- **Study Tour**: Short-term (days to weeks), professional/business exposure, credit or non-credit bearing

## Q5: Who can apply for exchange programmes?

- **Institutional-level**: All full-time undergraduate students from compatible disciplines
- **College-level**: Full-time undergraduate students in College of Computing programmes
- **Departmental-level**: Full-time undergraduate CS students (specific partner universities)

## Q6: When should I start planning for exchange?

Students should start planning early. There are normally **two rounds of applications** each academic year. Students are advised to:

1. Consult the **Departmental Exchange Coordinators** early
2. Check the **CS Student Exchange Canvas page** for useful information
3. Prepare for English language proficiency tests (IELTS/TOEFL) if needed

## Q7: What other international opportunities are available?

Besides exchange and study tours, CityUHK also offers:

- **Summer programmes** at partner institutions
- **CityUHK Discovery@World Programme** - tailored curriculums to individual locations
- **Semester at Sea** programme
- **Joint Bachelor's Degree Program** with Columbia University
- **Global Experience for ALL Funding Scheme** for financial support
