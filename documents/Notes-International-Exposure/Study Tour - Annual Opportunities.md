## Question

What study tours does CityUHK offer each year?

## Answer

## Annual Study Tour Opportunities

CityUHK offers various study tours throughout the academic year. While the specific tours may vary, here are the common types:

## Summer Study Tours

- **Summer study tours** are typically organized during the summer break
- These may include visits to organisations in various countries
- Students can gain professional exposure and cultural awareness

## College-Specific Study Tours

- **College of Business** - Business-focused study tours
- **College of Engineering** - Engineering-focused study tours
- **College of Science** - Science-focused study tours
- **College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences** - Humanities and social science study tours
- **School of Creative Media** - Creative media study tours
- **School of Energy and Environment** - Energy and environment study tours
- **School of Law** - Law study tours

## Department-Specific Study Tours

- **Electrical Engineering** - Engineering study tours
- **English** - Language and culture study tours
- **Linguistics and Translation** - Language and translation study tours
- **Public and International Affairs** - Public affairs study tours

## How to Find Study Tours

1. Check the **GEO website** for global opportunities
2. Check your **college/department website** for specific study tour announcements
3. Look for announcements via **student email** and **Canvas**
4. Contact the **Global Engagement Office (GEO)** for more information

## Key Facts

- Study tours are typically **short-term** (days to weeks)
- They are **credit or non-credit bearing**
- They focus on **professional business environments** and **cultural awareness**
- Skills developed include **leadership, interpersonal communication, interviewing, and presentation**
