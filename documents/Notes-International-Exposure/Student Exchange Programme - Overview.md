# Student Exchange Programme - Overview

## Question

What is the **Student Exchange Programme** at CityUHK, and how does it work for CS students?

## Answer

## What is Student Exchange?

Student exchange provides an opportunity for students to enrich their university life by studying and living outside Hong Kong at a partner institution for a period of time. It broadens students' international horizon and enriches their university education with cross-cultural exchange experience. During that period, students are registered as full-time students in overseas institutions. Credits earned can be transferred to the academic programmes students are studying.

Student exchange can be a valuable experience and play a vital role in the whole person development. The Department has received excellent feedback from students who have been on student exchange.

## Three Levels of Exchange Programmes

### 1. Institutional-Level Exchange Programmes

CityU has over **200 exchange agreements** with higher education institutions worldwide. All full-time undergraduate students enrolling at CityU from all compatible disciplines can apply for those programmes. The **Global Engagement Office (GEO)** is the central coordinating unit to provide a wide range of services and support to student exchange.

### 2. College-Level Exchange Programmes

The **College of Computing (CC)** has exchange agreements with higher education institutions overseas and in Chinese Mainland. All full-time undergraduate students enrolling in CC programmes of relevant disciplines are eligible for applying for those programmes.

### 3. Departmental-Level Exchange Programmes

The Department of Computer Science has signed student exchange agreements with the following universities for semester-long student exchange:

| Country | University |
|---------|-----------|
| Denmark | IT University of Copenhagen |
| France | EPITA - Graduate School of Computer Science |
| Spain | University of Alicante |

## Key Facts

- **Duration**: Semester-long (typically one semester)
- **Credit transfer**: Credits earned can be transferred back to CityUHK
- **Full-time status**: Students are registered as full-time students at the host institution
- **Eligibility**: Full-time undergraduate students in relevant disciplines
