# GE Requirements - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/curr/curr_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the frequently asked questions about **Gateway Education (GE) requirements** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q: What is Gateway Education (GE)?

Gateway Education (GE) is a set of general education requirements that all CityUHK students must complete as part of their degree. It provides a broad foundation in areas such as English, Chinese, and other general education subjects.

## Q: How many GE credit units do I need?

The GE requirement varies by admission category and admission year:

| Admission Category | GE Requirement (2025/26+) | GE Requirement (2024/25 and before) |
|--------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Normative 4-Year** | 31 credit units | 30 credit units |
| **Advanced Standing I** | 22 credit units | 21 credit units |
| **Advanced Standing II** | 12 credit units | 12 credit units |

## Q: What is the GE English requirement?

The GE English requirement is **6 credit units**. These credits are not counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

## Q: What is the GE Chinese requirement?

The GE Chinese requirement is **3 credit units**. These credits are not counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

## Q: Are GE English and Chinese credits counted towards graduation?

No. The GE English (6 credit units) and GE Chinese (3 credit units) credits earned will **not** be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will **not** be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

## Q: Is the GE requirement part of the graduation requirements?

Yes. The GE requirement is part of the overall graduation requirements. It is listed alongside the College/School Requirement, Major Requirement, Minor (Optional), and Free Electives.
