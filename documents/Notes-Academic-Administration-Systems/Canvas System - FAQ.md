# Canvas System - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/l/learning-management-system
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the Canvas system at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q: What is Canvas?

Canvas is the current unified learning management system (LMS) at CityUHK, provided by **Instructure, Inc.** It enables teachers and students to interact for learning and teaching purposes.

## Q: Where is Canvas hosted?

CityUHK Canvas is hosted by **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** in Singapore under a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model.

## Q: How often is Canvas updated?

Users are using the latest version of Canvas with rolling updates every **two weeks**.

## Q: How do I access Canvas?

- URL: https://canvas.cityu.edu.hk/
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID** and password
- You can also find the link on the CityUHK homepage under "Quick Links"

## Q: What will I see after logging in?

- **Teachers** will see the courses they are teaching
- **Students** will see what courses they have registered for

## Q: When are course sites created?

Course sites are created based on the **Master Class Schedule** before every semester to facilitate learning and teaching activities online.

## Q: Is Canvas the same as AIMS?

No. **Canvas** is the learning management system (LMS) for course content and learning activities. **AIMS** is the Administrative Information Management System for academic and administrative services (course registration, grades, etc.).

## Q: Can I access Canvas on my mobile device?

Canvas is a web-based system that can be accessed through a browser on any device. The Canvas mobile app is also available for iOS and Android devices.
