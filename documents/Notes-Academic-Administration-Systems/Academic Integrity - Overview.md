# Academic Integrity - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/pvdp/ah/ah-rules.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **academic honesty / integrity rules** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Academic Honesty Policy

CityUHK is committed to upholding **academic honesty**. All students are required to observe the **Rules on Academic Honesty** and to maintain the highest standards of academic integrity in all their academic work.

## What Constitutes Academic Dishonesty

Academic dishonesty includes, but is not limited to:

- **Plagiarism** — presenting someone else's work, ideas, or words as your own without proper acknowledgement
- **Collusion** — unauthorised collaboration with others on assessed work
- **Contract cheating** — commissioning or purchasing academic work from a third party
- **Fabrication** — falsifying data, results, or references
- **Duplication** — submitting the same work for more than one course without permission
- **Misuse of AI tools** — using generative AI in ways that violate course or University rules

## Use of Generative AI

- Students must follow the **course-specific rules** on the use of generative AI tools
- Where AI use is permitted, it must be **acknowledged** appropriately
- Unauthorised use of AI to produce assessed work is treated as academic dishonesty

## Online Tutorial and Quiz

- All students are required to complete the **Online Tutorial on Academic Honesty** and pass the associated **Quiz**
- This is a mandatory requirement for all students

## Penalties for Academic Dishonesty

Penalties for academic dishonesty can include:

- **Failing the course** or assessment
- **Suspension** from the University
- **Expulsion** from the University
- **Debarment** from graduation or award of a degree
- **Revocation** of a degree already awarded

## Where to Find the Rules

- The full **Rules on Academic Honesty** are published on the CityUHK website
- Students should familiarise themselves with the rules and consult their instructors if in doubt
