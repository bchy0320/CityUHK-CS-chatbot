# Final Year Project - FAQ

> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the frequently asked questions about the **Final Year Project (FYP)** at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## Q: What is the Final Year Project (FYP)?

The FYP is a capstone project undertaken by final-year undergraduate students in the BSc Computer Science programme. It allows students to apply the knowledge and skills acquired throughout their studies to a substantial, self-directed project under the supervision of an academic staff member.

## Q: When does the FYP take place?

The FYP takes place in the final year of study. The curriculum "devotes one year to a supervised work experience" to help students integrate and practise principles and techniques learned in the classroom.

## Q: How is the FYP supervised?

Each student is assigned a supervisor (academic staff member) who guides the project. The supervisor provides guidance on the project topic, methodology, and progress.

## Q: What is the FYP Showcase?

The FYP Showcase is an annual exhibition where final-year students present their projects. It features a vibrant display of projects spanning multiple disciplines within the Computer Science realm. The event allows academic staff to engage directly with students and explore thought-provoking ideas at the forefront of the field.

## Q: What are the Best Poster Awards?

The Best Poster Awards are presented at the FYP Exhibition to celebrate excellence in both research content and visual communication. They are evaluated by academic staff for clarity, originality, and impact.

## Q: When is the FYP Exhibition held?

- **2026**: 22 April 2026
- **2025**: 9 April 2025

## Q: Can the FYP help with postgraduate studies?

Yes. For students who desire to pursue postgraduate studies or a career in research and development, the FYP is one of several channels (alongside the Research Mentoring Scheme and guided studies) for undergraduate research. The demonstrated ability of students to innovate is a key factor in the success of our students in gaining admission to top tier postgraduate programs.
