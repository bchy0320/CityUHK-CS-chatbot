# Course Registration & Add-Drop - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/creg/wadp_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

Frequently asked questions about **course registration and add/drop** at CityUHK.

## Answer

## When can I add/drop courses?

- **Web-enabled courses** can be added/dropped online on AIMS during your designated **web registration time-ticket** period
- **Non-web-enabled courses** require the course-offering academic unit's permission via the electronic form on AIMS

## What is a registration time-ticket?

- Each student is assigned a **web registration time-ticket** which specifies the date/time window during which you can perform online add/drop of web-enabled courses
- Please refer to your web registration time-tickets for the exact dates

## What are Registration Restrictions?

- Course-offering academic units may specify **Registration Restrictions** (e.g., programme, year of study, prerequisites) for their courses
- To add a course that you cannot pass through the restrictions, you must obtain the academic unit's permission

## What is a CRN?

- **CRN** (Course Reference Number) is the unique identifier for a course section
- You use the CRN to add a specific course section on AIMS

## What is a waitlist?

- If a course section is full, you may be able to join a **waitlist**
- You will be enrolled automatically if a place becomes available, subject to the registration restrictions

## What happens if I drop a course late?

- After the add/drop deadline, a late drop is only approved by the **Head of the course-offering academic unit** under exceptional circumstances
- An **"X" grade** will be assigned and shown on your transcript

## Can I exceed the maximum study load?

- You may apply to **exceed the maximum study load** (18 credit units for full-time) via the electronic form on AIMS
- Approval is subject to the course-offering academic unit and your academic standing

## Can I reduce my study load?

- You may apply for a **reduced study load** via the electronic form on AIMS
- Approval is subject to the course-offering academic unit and your academic standing

## What is prohibited regarding add/drop?

- Students must not use any **unfair advantage** in the course registration process
- The University may take disciplinary action against students who attempt to gain unfair advantage in add/drop

## How do I access course registration?

- Log in to **AIMS** using your **CityUHK EID**
- Go to \[Course Registration > Web Add/Drop > Add or Drop Classes\]
