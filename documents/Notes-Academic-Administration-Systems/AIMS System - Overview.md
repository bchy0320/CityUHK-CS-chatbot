# AIMS System - Overview

## Question

What is the **AIMS (Administrative Information Management System)** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## What is AIMS?

**AIMS** stands for **Administrative Information Management System** at CityUHK. It is a suite of university information systems allowing students, staff and alumni access to a variety of essential academic support and administrative services. All AIMS functions are web-based and primarily developed for self-service purposes.

## What is Banner?

**Banner** is an Oracle® enterprise database used by the University to store all student, staff and alumni data. It is the world's leading higher education enterprise resource planning (ERP) system used by the University to manage, process, and store student, staff and alumni data.

## AIMS Sub-systems

AIMS consists of several sub-systems:

| Sub-system | Users | Functions |
|------------|-------|-----------|
| **Student System** | Students | Submit online application, register courses, check class and examination timetables, check grades, apply various student services |
| **Human Resources System** | Staff | Submit leave application, get pay advice, apply for staff development courses and outside practice |
| **Alumni System** | Alumni | Subscribe special university services, maintain contact with classmates by posting personal news |
| **Departmental Administrative System** | Departments | Manage their own students, staff and alumni, from applicant selection, marks entering, invigilation duty assignment, to staff leave approval |
| **Central Administrative System** | Central admin | Cover all essential administrative tasks of academic support and administrative departments, including finance systems (general ledger, accounts payable and procurement) |

## How to Access AIMS

- Staff and students may use their **CityUHK EID** to log in to AIMS
- All AIMS functions are web-based
