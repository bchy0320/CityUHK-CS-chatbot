# Academic Calendar - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/impd/impd_year.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

Frequently asked questions about the **academic calendar** at CityUHK.

## Answer

## When does Semester A begin?

- Classes for **Semester A** begin in **August**
- The first day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses is also in August

## When does Semester B begin?

- Classes for **Semester B** begin in **January**
- The first day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses is also in January

## When does the Summer Term begin?

- Classes for the **Summer Term** begin in **June**
- The first day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses is also in June

## When is the last day for course add/drop?

| Semester/Term | Last Day for Add/Drop |
|---------------|----------------------|
| **Semester A** | September |
| **Semester B** | January |
| **Summer Term** | June |

## When are tuition fees due?

- **Semester A** tuition fees: due in August (balance due in September)
- **Semester B** tuition fees: due in January (balance due in February)
- **Summer Term** tuition fees: due in July

## When is the examination period?

| Semester/Term | Examination Period |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Semester A** | December |
| **Semester B** | April |
| **Summer Term** | August |

## When are course grades released?

| Semester/Term | Grades Released |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Semester A** | December |
| **Semester B** | May |
| **Summer Term** | August |

## When can I apply for graduation?

- **Semester A**: September
- **Semester B**: January
- **Summer Term**: June

## When can I apply for credit transfer / course exemption?

- **Semester A**: July
- **Semester B**: December

## What are the tuition fee charges for late withdrawal/drop?

- **No tuition fee charged**: before the 50% fee date
- **50% tuition fee charged**: after the no-fee period
- **100% tuition fee charged**: after the 50% fee date
