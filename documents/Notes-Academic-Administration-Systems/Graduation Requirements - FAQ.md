# Graduation Requirements - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/curr/curr_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the frequently asked questions about **graduation requirements** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q: What is the minimum number of credit units required to graduate?

The minimum graduation requirements vary by admission category:
- **Normative 4-Year**: 120 credit units
- **Advanced Standing I**: 90 credit units
- **Advanced Standing II**: 60 credit units

## Q: What is the maximum number of credit units I can take?

The maximum credit units permitted also vary by admission category:
- **Normative 4-Year**: 144 credit units
- **Advanced Standing I**: 114 credit units
- **Advanced Standing II**: 84 credit units

## Q: What is the College/School and Major requirement?

The College/School Requirement and Major Requirement is **54-72 credit units**, with the major not less than 27 credit units.

## Q: What is the Gateway Education (GE) requirement?

The GE requirement varies by admission year:
- **Normative 4-Year**: 31 credit units (2025/26 and thereafter) or 30 credit units (2024/25 and before)
- **Advanced Standing I**: 22 credit units (2025/26 and thereafter) or 21 credit units (2024/25 and before)
- **Advanced Standing II**: 12 credit units

## Q: Are GE English and Chinese credits counted towards graduation?

No. The GE English (6 credit units) and GE Chinese (3 credit units) credits earned will **not** be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will **not** be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

## Q: Is a Minor required for graduation?

No. A Minor is optional (15-18 credit units).

## Q: What are Free Electives?

Free Electives are the remainder of credit units needed to fulfill graduation requirements after completing the College/School, Major, GE, and Minor requirements.

## Q: How do I apply for graduation?

Submit an **Application for Graduation** in AIMS during the designated application period. Check DegreeWorks first to verify that you have met all graduation requirements.

## Q: What credit units are required to apply for graduation?

- **Normative 4-Year**: 90+ credit units
- **Advanced Standing I**: 60+ credit units
- **Advanced Standing II**: 33+ credit units
