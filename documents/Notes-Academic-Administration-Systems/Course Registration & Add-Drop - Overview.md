# Course Registration & Add-Drop - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/creg/creg_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

How does **course registration and add/drop** work at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Course Registration

Course registration at CityUHK is handled through the **AIMS** system. Students register for courses each semester, and most courses are **web-enabled** so they can be added/dropped online.

## Study Load Limits

| Student Type | Minimum | Maximum |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| **Full-time** (per semester) | 12 credit units | 18 credit units |
| **Part-time** (per semester) | - | 11 credit units |
| **Summer Term** (all students) | - | 7 credit units |

## Pre-registration Schedule

- Your **pre-registration schedule** is mainly compiled with the essential courses for fulfilling your degree requirements
- You may use it as a draft version to further compile your class schedule in accordance with your study plan
- You are advised to meet your **Advisor** before making any adjustments to the pre-registration schedule
- When your course grades for the previous semester are released, you might need to make adjustments to your class schedule

## Web Add/Drop

Most courses are **web-enabled** and can be added/dropped on AIMS \[Course Registration > Web Add/Drop > Add or Drop Classes\] within the designated registration time-ticket (period) directly, if they can pass through the **Registration Restrictions** specified by course-offering academic units.

- To add/drop courses that are **non-web-enabled** or to bypass registration restrictions, the course-offering academic unit's permission must be obtained via the electronic form on AIMS \[Course Registration > Application for Add/Drop of Non-Web-enabled Course & Study Load Adjustment\]

## Late Add/Drop

- After the add/drop deadline and before the end of teaching period, any request for add/drop of courses will only be approved by the **Head of the course-offering academic unit** under exceptional circumstances
- For approved late drop request, an **"X" grade** will be assigned for the course and shown on the student's transcript
- Late requests for dropping courses must be submitted no later than the end of the teaching period for the relevant semester/term

## De-registration

The University reserves the right to **de-register students** from a course or course section(s) under certain circumstances.

## How to Access

- Log in to **AIMS** using your **CityUHK EID**
- Course registration functions are under \[Course Registration\]
