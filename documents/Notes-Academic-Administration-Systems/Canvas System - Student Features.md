## Question

What **student features** are available in the Canvas system at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Student Features in Canvas

Canvas is the unified learning management system (LMS) at CityUHK. Students can use Canvas to:

### Course Access

- **View registered courses** - After logging in, students will see the courses they have registered for
- **Access course materials** - View course content, lecture notes, and resources posted by teachers
- **View course announcements** - Check announcements from teachers about course updates

### Learning & Teaching Interaction

- **Submit assignments** - Submit coursework and assignments online
- **Take quizzes and tests** - Complete online quizzes and assessments
- **Participate in discussions** - Engage in online discussions with classmates and teachers
- **View grades** - Check grades for assignments, quizzes, and exams
- **Access course calendar** - View important dates and deadlines for each course

### Communication

- **Message teachers** - Send messages to teachers through Canvas
- **Collaborate with classmates** - Work on group projects and collaborate online

## How to Access

- URL: https://canvas.cityu.edu.hk/
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID** and password
- After logging in, students will see the courses they have registered for
