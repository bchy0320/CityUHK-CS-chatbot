# CityUHK Portal - Overview

## Question

What is the **CityUHK Portal**?

## Answer

## What is CityUHK Portal?

The **CityUHK Portal** is a one-stop platform for staff and students to access frequently used functions and to obtain up-to-date information. The functions and services information is categorised by type of services.

## Service Categories

The functions and services information is categorised by type of services, including:

- **News and Media** - Up-to-date news and announcements
- **Staff Admin** - Staff administrative functions
- **Student Life** - Student life related services
- **Academic & Research** - Academic and research related information and services (e.g. Academic Calendar, Student/Faculty Schedule)
- **IT and Library Services** - IT and library related services
- **Campus Facilities** - Various facilities booking
- **Policies & Useful Information** - University policies and useful information

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Widget** | Integrates with other systems, such as AIMS, CityUHK Announcement Portal (CAP), CityUHK Homepage, YouTube, and Hong Kong Observatory |
| **Search function** | Search bar to find required functions quickly |
| **Dark theme option** | Toggle between dark theme and light theme |
| **Responsive design** | Fits different screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile) |
| **My Dashboard** | Allows users to place their favourite functions and services on a single page |

## How to Access

- URL: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/portal/
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID**
