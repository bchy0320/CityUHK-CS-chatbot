# GPA & CGPA - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/asmt/cgrd_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

Frequently asked questions about **GPA / CGPA** at CityUHK.

## Answer

## What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?

- **SGPA** is your Grade Point Average for a single semester
- **CGPA** is your Cumulative Grade Point Average across all semesters of study

## How is GPA calculated?

- GPA = **total grade points earned ÷ total credit units attempted**
- Grade points = **grade point value × credit units** of the course
- GPA is **truncated to 2 decimal places** (not rounded)

## What is the grade point scale?

| Grade | Grade Points |
|-------|--------------|
| **A+** | 4.3 |
| **A** | 4.0 |
| **A-** | 3.7 |
| **B+** | 3.3 |
| **B** | 3.0 |
| **B-** | 2.7 |
| **C+** | 2.3 |
| **C** | 2.0 |
| **C-** | 1.7 |
| **D** | 1.0 |
| **F** | 0.0 |

## What happens if I repeat a course?

- The **latest grade** is used in the GPA calculation
- The previous grade remains on the transcript but is not counted in the GPA

## What is the Dean's List?

- Students with a **GPA of 3.7 or above** in a semester, with **12 or more credit units** and **no failures**, may be placed on the **Dean's List**

## What are the award classifications?

| Classification | CGPA |
|----------------|------|
| **First Class Honours** | 3.50 or above |
| **Upper Second Class Honours** | 3.00 – 3.49 |
| **Lower Second Class Honours** | 2.50 – 2.99 |
| **Third Class Honours** | 2.00 – 2.49 |
| **Pass** | 1.70 – 1.99 |

## What is academic standing?

| Standing | CGPA |
|----------|------|
| **Good Standing** | 2.00 or above |
| **Academic Warning** | Below 2.00 |
| **Academic Probation** | Below 1.70 (or as specified) |
| **Academic Suspension** | Below the probation threshold |

## Where can I view my GPA?

- View your grades and GPA in **AIMS** under \[Course Registration > Course Grades\]
