## Question

What is the **CityUHK Mobile App**?

## Answer

## What is CityUHK Mobile?

**CityUHK Mobile** is an official mobile app developed by the Central IT. Being one of the top-rated University apps in Hong Kong, CityUHK Mobile provides a convenient platform for staff, students and visitors to access important information about the University.

## Who Can Use It?

- **Staff** - Access personalised information
- **Students** - Access personalised information
- **Visitors** - Access general university information

## Personalised Information (for logged-in staff & students)

Staff and students who log into the mobile app will be able to access more personalised information such as:

- Class schedule
- Examination schedule
- Library loan records
- And more

## Highlighted Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Schedule** | Check when and where to attend your classes |
| **Room Booking** | Check the availability of rooms, e.g. classrooms, meeting rooms and study rooms |
| **Staff Directory** | Enhanced staff search functionalities for staff and students |
| **Places** | Provides a list of points of interest in the university including canteens, library and printing area |
