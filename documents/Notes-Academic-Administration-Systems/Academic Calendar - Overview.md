# Academic Calendar - Overview

## Question

How does the **academic calendar** work at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Academic Year Structure

The academic year at CityUHK consists of:

- **Semester A** (typically September – December)
- **Semester B** (typically January – May)
- **Summer Term** (typically June – August)

## Key Dates for Semester A

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| First day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses | August |
| Classes for Semester A begin | August |
| Due date for Semester A's tuition fees | August |
| Last day for course add/drop in Semester A | September |
| Application for graduation in Semester A | September |
| Last day to apply for reduced/exceeding study load, withdrawal, leave of absence, late drop (no fee) | September |
| 50% tuition fee charged for withdrawal/leave/late drop | September |
| Due date for payment of balance of Semester A's tuition fees | September |
| 100% tuition fee charged for withdrawal/leave/late drop | October |
| Release of Personal Class Schedule for Semester B | November |
| Release of Semester A's examination schedule | November |
| Last day of teaching for Semester A | November |
| Last day to apply for late leave of absence and late drop | November |
| Last day to apply for withdrawal of studies | December |
| Examination period for Semester A | December |
| Release of Semester A's course grades | December |

## Key Dates for Semester B

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| First day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses | January |
| Classes for Semester B begin | January |
| Due date for Semester B's tuition fees | January |
| Last day for course add/drop in Semester B | January |
| Application for graduation in Semester B | January |
| Last day to apply for reduced/exceeding study load, withdrawal, leave, late drop (no fee) | January |
| 50% tuition fee charged for withdrawal/leave/late drop | January |
| Due date for payment of balance of Semester B's tuition fees | February |
| 100% tuition fee charged for withdrawal/leave/late drop | March |
| Release of Semester B's examination schedule | March |
| Last day of teaching for Semester B | April |
| Examination period for Semester B | April |
| Release of Semester B's course grades | May |

## Key Dates for Summer Term

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| First day to apply for add/drop of non-web-enabled courses, exceeding max load | May |
| First day for online add/drop of web-enabled courses | June |
| Classes for Summer Term begin | June |
| Last day for course add/drop in Summer Term | June |
| Application for graduation in Summer Term | June |
| Last day to apply for exceeding max load, withdrawal, leave, late drop | June |
| Due date for Summer Term's tuition fees | July |
| Release of Summer Term's examination schedule | July |
| Last day of teaching for Summer Term | July |
| Examination period for Summer Term | August |
| Release of Summer Term's course grades | August |

## Other Important Dates

- **Application for credit transfer/course exemption** in Semester A: July
- **Application for credit transfer/course exemption** in Semester B: December
- **Declaring a home major** (for undeclared-major students): May
- **Change of home major / add-drop of second major / add-drop of minors**: June (Summer) and November (Semester A)
