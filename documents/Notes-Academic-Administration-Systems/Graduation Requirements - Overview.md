# Graduation Requirements - Overview

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/curr/curr_main.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **graduation requirements** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Minimum Graduation Requirements

The minimum graduation requirements are subject to the requirements of individual Colleges/Schools. The requirements vary depending on the student's admission category.

### Normative 4-Year Degree

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Minimum Graduation Requirements** | 120 credit units |
| **Maximum Credit Units Permitted** | 144 credit units |
| **College/School Requirement and Major Requirement** (major should not be less than 27 credit units) | 54 - 72 credit units |
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 31 credit units* |
| **GE - English** | 6 credit units# |
| **GE - Chinese** | 3 credit units# |
| **Minor (Optional)** | 15 - 18 credit units |
| **Free Electives** | Remainder to fulfill graduation requirements |

\*The Gateway Education Requirement of 31 credit units only applies to students admitted in 2025/26 and thereafter. For students admitted in 2024/25 and before, the Gateway Education requirement is 30 credit units.

#The credits earned will not be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

### Advanced Standing I

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Minimum Graduation Requirements** | 90 credit units |
| **Maximum Credit Units Permitted** | 114 credit units |
| **College/School Requirement and Major Requirement** (major should not be less than 27 credit units) | 54 - 72 credit units |
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 22 credit units* |
| **GE - English** | 6 credit units# |
| **GE - Chinese** | 3 credit units# |
| **Minor (Optional)** | 15 - 18 credit units |
| **Free Electives** | Remainder to fulfill graduation requirements |

\*The Gateway Education Requirement of 22 credit units only applies to students admitted in 2025/26 and thereafter. For students admitted in 2024/25 and before, the Gateway Education requirement is 21 credit units.

#The credits earned will not be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

### Advanced Standing II

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Minimum Graduation Requirements** | 60 credit units |
| **Maximum Credit Units Permitted** | 84 credit units |
| **College/School Requirement and Major Requirement** (major should not be less than 27 credit units) | 54 - 72 credit units |
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 12 credit units |
| **Minor (Optional)** | 15 - 18 credit units |
| **Free Electives** | Remainder to fulfill graduation requirements |

## Key Points

- **Minimum graduation requirements** vary by admission category (120 / 90 / 60 credit units)
- **Maximum credit units permitted** also vary (144 / 114 / 84 credit units)
- **College/School + Major requirement** is 54-72 credit units, with the major not less than 27 credit units
- **GE requirement** varies by admission year (31/30 for normative, 22/21 for Advanced Standing I, 12 for Advanced Standing II)
- **GE English and Chinese** credits are not counted towards minimum graduation requirements or CGPA, but count towards maximum credit units permitted
- **Minor** is optional (15-18 credit units)
- **Free electives** fill the remainder
