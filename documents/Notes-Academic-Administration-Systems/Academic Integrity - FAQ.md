# Academic Integrity - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/pvdp/ah/ah-rules.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

Frequently asked questions about **academic integrity** at CityUHK.

## Answer

## What is plagiarism?

- **Plagiarism** is presenting someone else's work, ideas, or words as your own without proper acknowledgement
- This includes copying text, ideas, or data from any source without citation

## What is collusion?

- **Collusion** is unauthorised collaboration with others on assessed work
- Working with classmates on an individual assignment without permission is collusion

## What is contract cheating?

- **Contract cheating** is commissioning or purchasing academic work from a third party
- This includes paying someone to write your assignment or take your exam

## Can I use AI tools for my coursework?

- You must follow the **course-specific rules** on the use of generative AI
- Where AI use is permitted, it must be **acknowledged** appropriately
- Unauthorised use of AI to produce assessed work is treated as academic dishonesty

## Do I need to complete the Online Tutorial on Academic Honesty?

- **Yes**, all students are required to complete the **Online Tutorial on Academic Honesty** and pass the associated **Quiz**

## What are the penalties for academic dishonesty?

| Penalty | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Fail the course** | Failing the course or assessment |
| **Suspension** | Suspension from the University |
| **Expulsion** | Expulsion from the University |
| **Debarment** | Debarment from graduation or award of a degree |
| **Revocation** | Revocation of a degree already awarded |

## How do I avoid academic dishonesty?

- Always **cite** your sources properly
- Do not share your work with others for individual assignments
- Follow the course rules on AI use
- Ask your instructor if you are unsure about what is permitted

## Where can I find the full rules?

- The full **Rules on Academic Honesty** are published on the CityUHK website
- Consult your instructors or the Academic Regulations and Records Office if in doubt
