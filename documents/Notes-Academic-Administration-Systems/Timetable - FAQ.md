# Timetable - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/arro/exam/exam_ttbl.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

Frequently asked questions about the **timetable / class schedule** at CityUHK.

## Answer

## Where can I find my class schedule?

- Your **Personal Class Schedule** is available in **AIMS** under \[Course Registration > Personal Class Schedule\]
- It is released for each semester/term

## When is the Master Class Schedule released?

- The **Master Class Schedule** for the academic year is released in **July** in AIMS

## When is my Personal Class Schedule released?

| Semester/Term | Release Time |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Semester A** | July |
| **Semester B** | November |
| **Summer Term** | April |

## What are the class hours?

- Classes are normally held **Monday to Friday**, from **9:00 am to 6:50 pm**, with evening classes from **7:00 pm to 10:50 pm**

## What is a CRN?

- **CRN** (Course Reference Number) is the unique identifier for a course section
- It is used to identify and add a specific course section

## What does "web-enabled" mean?

- A **web-enabled** course can be added/dropped online on AIMS during your registration time-ticket
- **Non-web-enabled** courses require the course-offering academic unit's permission

## When is the examination schedule released?

| Semester/Term | Release Time |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Semester A** | November |
| **Semester B** | March |
| **Summer Term** | July |

## Where can I find exam venues?

- Exam venues are shown in the **examination schedule** in AIMS
- The exam timetable is released in AIMS for each semester/term

## Can I change my class schedule?

- You can add/drop courses during the registration period to change your class schedule
- After the add/drop deadline, changes require the course-offering academic unit's approval
