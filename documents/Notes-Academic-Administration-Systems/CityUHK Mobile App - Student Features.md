## Question

What **student features** are available in the CityUHK Mobile App?

## Answer

## Student Features in CityUHK Mobile

CityUHK Mobile provides a convenient platform for students to access important information about the University. Students who log into the mobile app will be able to access more personalised information.

### Personalised Information

- **Class schedule** - Check when and where to attend your classes
- **Examination schedule** - Check your exam timetable
- **Library loan records** - View your library borrowing records

### Highlighted Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Schedule** | Check when and where to attend your classes |
| **Room Booking** | Check the availability of rooms, e.g. classrooms, meeting rooms and study rooms |
| **Staff Directory** | Enhanced staff search functionalities for staff and students |
| **Places** | Provides a list of points of interest in the university including canteens, library and printing area |

## How to Access

- Download the **CityUHK Mobile** app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID** to access personalised information
