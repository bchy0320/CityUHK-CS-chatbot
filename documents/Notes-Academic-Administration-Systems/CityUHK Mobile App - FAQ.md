# CityUHK Mobile App - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/c/cityuhk-mobile-app
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the CityUHK Mobile App?

## Answer

## Q: What is CityUHK Mobile?

CityUHK Mobile is an official mobile app developed by the Central IT. It is one of the top-rated University apps in Hong Kong.

## Q: Who can use CityUHK Mobile?

- **Staff** - Access personalised information
- **Students** - Access personalised information
- **Visitors** - Access general university information

## Q: What personalised information can I access after logging in?

Staff and students who log into the mobile app will be able to access:
- Class schedule
- Examination schedule
- Library loan records
- And more

## Q: What are the highlighted features?

- **Schedule** - Check when and where to attend your classes
- **Room Booking** - Check the availability of rooms, e.g. classrooms, meeting rooms and study rooms
- **Staff Directory** - Enhanced staff search functionalities for staff and students
- **Places** - Provides a list of points of interest in the university including canteens, library and printing area

## Q: How do I download CityUHK Mobile?

Download the **CityUHK Mobile** app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

## Q: Do I need to log in to use CityUHK Mobile?

Visitors can access general university information without logging in. Staff and students who log in will be able to access more personalised information such as class schedule, examination schedule, and library loan records.
