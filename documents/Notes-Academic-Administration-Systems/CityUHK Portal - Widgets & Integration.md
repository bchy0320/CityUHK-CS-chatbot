## Question

What **widgets and integrations** are available in the CityUHK Portal?

## Answer

## Widgets & Integration

"Widget" is introduced into the CityUHK Portal for integrating with other systems, such as:

- **AIMS** - Administrative Information Management System
- **CityUHK Announcement Portal (CAP)** - University announcements
- **CityUHK Homepage** - Latest news from NewsCentre
- **YouTube** - Latest videos published in CityUTube Channel
- **Hong Kong Observatory** - Weather information

## Examples of Widgets

### News & Media Page

- **"CITYUHK NEWS" Widget** - Integrated with CityUHK Homepage to retrieve the latest news from NewsCentre
- **"CITYUTUBE" Widget** - Integrated with YouTube to show the latest videos published in CityUTube Channel
- **"MY CAP MESSAGES" Widget** - Integrated with the CityUHK Announcement Portal to obtain the recent announcements for you

### Widgets Under Development

Various widgets, such as:
- "Academic Honesty"
- "My TLQ"
- "My Library"
- "My IT"

...are under development and will be available soon.
