## Question

How do I **apply for graduation** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Application for Graduation

Students who expect to complete their degree requirements must submit an **Application for Graduation** during the designated application period.

## How to Apply

1. **Submit the application in AIMS** during the application period
2. **Check DegreeWorks** first to verify that you have met all graduation requirements
3. **Ensure you have completed the required credit units** for your admission category

## Required Credit Units

| Admission Category | Required Credit Units |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| **Normative 4-Year** | 90+ credit units |
| **Advanced Standing I** | 60+ credit units |
| **Advanced Standing II** | 33+ credit units |

## Important Notes

- The application must be submitted during the designated application period
- Check DegreeWorks to verify you have met all requirements before applying
- The required credit units vary by admission category
