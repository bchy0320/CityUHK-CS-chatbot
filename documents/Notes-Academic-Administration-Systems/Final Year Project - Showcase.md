## Question

What is the **Final Year Project (FYP) Showcase** at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## What is the FYP Showcase?

The **Final Year Project (FYP) Showcase** is an annual exhibition that showcases the outstanding creativity, innovation, and research achievements of the graduating cohort. The event allows academic staff to engage directly with students and explore thought-provoking ideas at the forefront of the field.

## FYP Exhibition 2026

The 2026 edition of the FYP Exhibition was successfully held on **22 April 2026**. It featured a vibrant display of projects spanning multiple disciplines within the Computer Science realm.

### Best Poster Awards
