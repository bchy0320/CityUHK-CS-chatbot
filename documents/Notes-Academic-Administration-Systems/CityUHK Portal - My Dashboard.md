## Question

What is **My Dashboard** in the CityUHK Portal?

## Answer

## What is My Dashboard?

"My Dashboard" facilitates users to place their favourite functions and services in a single page. Staff and students are free to customize the dashboard themselves to fit their personal needs.

## How to Use My Dashboard

### Default Dashboard

When you first login to the new CityUHK Portal, a default dashboard will be generated for you.

### Adding Items to My Dashboard

You may add your favourite items and widgets of different Portal pages to "My Dashboard" by clicking:

- The **"Pin" icon** of a menu item
- The **"Star" icon** of a widget

### Pinning Search Results

You may also pin an item of search results to "My Dashboard" by clicking the corresponding "Pin" icon on the right.
