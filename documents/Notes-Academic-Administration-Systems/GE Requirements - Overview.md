## Question

What are the **Gateway Education (GE) requirements** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## What is Gateway Education (GE)?

**Gateway Education (GE)** is a set of general education requirements that all CityUHK students must complete as part of their degree. It provides a broad foundation in areas such as English, Chinese, and other general education subjects.

## GE Requirements by Admission Category

### Normative 4-Year Degree

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 31 credit units* |
| **GE - English** | 6 credit units# |
| **GE - Chinese** | 3 credit units# |

\*The Gateway Education Requirement of 31 credit units only applies to students admitted in 2025/26 and thereafter. For students admitted in 2024/25 and before, the Gateway Education requirement is 30 credit units.

#The credits earned will not be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

### Advanced Standing I

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 22 credit units* |
| **GE - English** | 6 credit units# |
| **GE - Chinese** | 3 credit units# |

\*The Gateway Education Requirement of 22 credit units only applies to students admitted in 2025/26 and thereafter. For students admitted in 2024/25 and before, the Gateway Education requirement is 21 credit units.

#The credits earned will not be counted towards the minimum credit units required for graduation and will not be included in the calculation of cumulative grade point average (CGPA). They will be counted towards the maximum credit units permitted.

### Advanced Standing II

| Requirement | Credit Units |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Gateway Education (GE) Requirement** | 12 credit units |

## Key Points

- **GE requirement** varies by admission category and admission year
- **GE English** (6 credit units) and **GE Chinese** (3 credit units) are separate from the main GE requirement
- **GE English and Chinese credits** are not counted towards minimum graduation requirements or CGPA, but count towards maximum credit units permitted
- The GE requirement is part of the overall graduation requirements
