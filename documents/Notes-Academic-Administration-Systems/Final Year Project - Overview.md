## Question

What is the **Final Year Project (FYP)** in the BSc Computer Science programme at CityUHK?

## Answer

## What is the Final Year Project?

The **Final Year Project (FYP)** is a capstone project undertaken by final-year undergraduate students in the BSc Computer Science programme. It allows students to apply the knowledge and skills acquired throughout their studies to a substantial, self-directed project.

## Role in the Curriculum

The FYP is part of the curriculum that "devotes one year to a supervised work experience to help students integrate and practise principles and techniques learned in the classroom and develop the necessary maturity and soft skills essential for a successful career in information technology."

## Purpose

The FYP serves several purposes:

- **Integration**: Allows students to integrate knowledge from core courses and electives into a single project
- **Research exposure**: Introduces students to a research culture through supervised project work
- **Career preparation**: Develops project management, communication, and technical skills needed for professional careers
- **Postgraduate pathway**: For students who desire to pursue postgraduate studies or a career in research and development, the FYP is one of several channels (alongside the Research Mentoring Scheme and guided studies) for undergraduate research

## Key Features

- **Supervised**: Each student is assigned a supervisor (academic staff member) who guides the project
- **Self-directed**: Students choose or are assigned a project topic and work on it independently
- **Showcase**: The FYP culminates in an exhibition where students present their work
