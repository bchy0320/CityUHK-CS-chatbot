# AIMS System - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/esu/aims.htm
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the AIMS system at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q: What does AIMS stand for?

**AIMS** stands for **Administrative Information Management System** at CityUHK.

## Q: What is the difference between AIMS and Banner?

- **Banner** is an Oracle® enterprise database used by the University to store all student, staff and alumni data
- **AIMS** is a suite of university information systems allowing students, staff and alumni access to a variety of essential academic support and administrative services

## Q: Who can use AIMS?

- **Students** - Access class schedules, grades, add/drop courses, apply for credit transfer and transcripts
- **Staff** - Access employment information such as appointments, payroll and benefits, submit leave applications, apply for staff development courses
- **Alumni** - Subscribe special university services, maintain contact with classmates
- **Administrative staff** - Use a suite of online functions to assist in carrying out administrative duties

## Q: How do I log in to AIMS?

Use your **CityUHK EID** to log in to AIMS. All AIMS functions are web-based.

## Q: What can students do in AIMS?

Students can:
- View class schedules and grades
- Update contact information
- Add/drop courses
- Apply for credit transfer and transcripts online
- Apply various student services

## Q: What can staff do in AIMS?

Staff can:
- Access employment information such as appointments, payroll and benefits
- Update contact information
- Submit leave applications
- Apply for staff development courses online

## Q: Is AIMS available to alumni?

Yes. The **Alumni System** enables alumni to subscribe special university services and maintain contact with their classmates by posting personal news.

## Q: What is the Departmental Administrative System?

It provides functions for departments to manage their own students, staff and alumni, from applicant selection, marks entering, invigilation duty assignment, to staff leave approval. Other general departmental functions include procurement, development management and software asset management.
