# CityUHK Portal - FAQ

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/c/cityuhk-portal
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

What are the **frequently asked questions** about the CityUHK Portal?

## Answer

## Q: What is the CityUHK Portal?

The CityUHK Portal is a one-stop platform for staff and students to access frequently used functions and to obtain up-to-date information.

## Q: How do I access the CityUHK Portal?

- URL: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/portal/
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID**

## Q: What are the service categories in the Portal?

The functions and services information is categorised by type of services, including:
- News and Media
- Staff Admin
- Student Life
- Academic & Research
- IT and Library Services
- Campus Facilities
- Policies & Useful Information

## Q: What is My Dashboard?

"My Dashboard" facilitates users to place their favourite functions and services in a single page. Staff and students are free to customize the dashboard themselves to fit their personal needs.

## Q: What is the Widget feature?

"Widget" is introduced into the CityUHK Portal for integrating with other systems, such as AIMS, CityUHK Announcement Portal (CAP), CityUHK Homepage, YouTube, and Hong Kong Observatory.

## Q: Does the Portal have a search function?

Yes. A search function that facilitates users to look for their required function quickly is in place. Simply enter keyword(s) onto the Search bar on the top of the Portal, the list of related items will be shown.

## Q: Does the Portal support dark theme?

Yes. Dark Theme is available and users could click the dark theme switch located on the right of the Search bar to toggle between dark theme and light theme.

## Q: Is the Portal responsive?

Yes. The new CityUHK Portal is with responsive design to fit different screen sizes, including desktop, tablet, and mobile phones.
