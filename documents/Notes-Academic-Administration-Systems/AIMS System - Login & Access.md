# AIMS System - Login & Access

> [!tip] Source
> Extracted from: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/its/services-facilities/list-of-services-facilities/c/cityuhk-eid-authentication
> Fetched: 2026-10-30

## Question

How do I **log in to AIMS** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Login to AIMS

Staff and students may use their **CityUHK EID** to log in to AIMS.

## What is CityUHK EID?

**CityUHK Electronic ID (CityUHK EID)** is a unique ID automatically assigned to all staff and students for accessing IT services provided by the University, including:

- AIMS
- Canvas
- CAP (CityUHK Announcement Portal)
- Email
- Portal
- CityUHK Scholars
- CityUHKWiki

## How is the EID Assigned?

- **New students**: Their EIDs are the same as the ones assigned to them when they were applicants during the application process
- **New staff**: Their EIDs are assigned to them when they accept offers from the University
- Once assigned, the EID will be used by that person even when he/she changes his/her role in the University as a student, staff or alumnus

## EID Format

- The EID is generated and assigned in an **alpha-numeric format** based on the name of an individual user
- Some existing students and alumni are using their **Student Numbers** as their EIDs

### Example

For a new user, CHAN Tai Man, he may be assigned an EID of "**tmchan**" based on availability. This user should use **tmchan** to log in all IT services provided by the University.
