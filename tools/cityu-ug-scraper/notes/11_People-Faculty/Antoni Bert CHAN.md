BSc MEng Cornell, PhD UC San Diego, SMIEEE

Dr. Antoni Chan is a professor at the [City University of Hong Kong](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) in the [Department of Computer Science](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html). Before joining CityU, he was a postdoctoral researcher in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) at the [University of California, San Diego](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) (UC San Diego). He received the Ph.D. degree from UC San Diego in 2008 studying in the [Statistical and Visual Computing Lab](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) (SVCL). He received the B.Sc. and M.Eng. in [Electrical Engineering](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) from [Cornell University](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) in 2000 and 2001. From 2001 to 2003, he was a Visiting Scientist in the [Computer Vision and Image Analysis](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) lab at Cornell. In 2005, he was a summer intern at [Google](http://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/profile/abchan.html) in New York City.
