## JUPAS (HKDSE) - Entrance Requirements

### What are the entrance requirements ?

For admissions, JUPAS HKDSE applicants must meet the following entrance requirements.

**JS1204 BSc Computer Science & JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity**

| Subject | Minimum Level Required | 
|---|---|
| English Language | Level 3 | 
| Chinese Language | Level 3 | 
| Mathematics | Level 3 | 
| Citizenship and Social Development | Attained | 
| Elective 1\* | Level 3 in one elective subject from: **Biology / Chemistry / Information and Communication Technology / M1 / M2 / Physics** | 
| Elective 2\* | Level 3 in any elective subjects | 


**JS 1221 BSc in Computer Science and BSc in Computational Finance and Financial Technology** 

| Subject | Minimum Level Required | 
|---|---|
| English Language | Level 3 | 
| Chinese Language | Level 3 | 
| Mathematics | Level 4 | 
| Citizenship and Social Development | Attained | 
| Elective 1\* | Level 3 in one elective subject from: **Biology / Chemistry / Information and Communication Technology / M1 / M2 / Physics** | 
| Elective 2\* | Level 3 in any elective subjects | 

\* Notes:

1. Unspecified elective subjects may include "other languages" at grade E or above.
2. If students take both M1 and M2, they are counted as one subject only.
3. Applied Learning subjects are not counted as elective subjects.
