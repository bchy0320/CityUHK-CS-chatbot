## Direct Application - Admission Statistics

### What is the level of Competition for Direct applicants?

- As shown in the figures below, the competition for admission to BSc Computer Science (code: 1561A) through direct application is very keen and only **Good** students with **STRONG** motivation to study Computer Science are selected.
- In recent years, we received about 500 applications each year from direct application for BSc Computer Science (code: 1561A) and admitted around 20 students into the second year and 20 to 25 students into the senior (third) year.
