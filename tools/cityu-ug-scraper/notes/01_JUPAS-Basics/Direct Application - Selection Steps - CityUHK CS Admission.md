# Admission Process Timeline (BSc in Computer Science)

The process is divided into four main phases based on time periods, connected by flow arrows.

### Phase 1: Sep – Jan (Initial Application)
- **Direct Application**
  - Categories for applicants:
    - **HD/AD**
    - **Others**
    - **Non-local**

### Phase 2: Nov – May (Assessment & Interviews)
- **Admission Interviews**
- **Decision**
  - **Path A (Approved):** Proceeds to the next phase (Offer Lists).
  - **Path B (Rejected):** Continues to the **Reserve List**.

### Phase 3: Dec – Jul (Offers & Supplementary)
- **Offer List Year 1**
- **Offer List Advanced Standing I**
- **Offer List Advanced Standing II**
- **Top-up** (Connects directly to the final phase)

### Phase 4: Early Aug (Enrolment)
- **Enrolment** for the **BSc in Computer Science**
- **Orientation & Preparation for the Start of the Semester**
