## JUPAS (HKDSE) - JUPAS Interview

### Do JUPAS Applicants need to attend Admission Interviews ?

**Selected applicants** may be invited for admission interview after the announcement of HKDSE examination results in **late July**.

### Purpose

- To identify students' motivation for taking our major;
- To let the students to know more about our major and
- To evaluate students' suitability for our major based on personal characters and non-academic performance.

### Performance in Admission Interview

- OUTSTANDING or GOOD: The selection priorities will be increased.
- AVERAGE or BELOW AVERAGE: The selection priorities will be solely based on the DSE results and band choices.

### Admissions Interviews

Shortlisted applicants will be invited to interview. You may watch the video below to learn more about how to prepare for admissions interviews.
