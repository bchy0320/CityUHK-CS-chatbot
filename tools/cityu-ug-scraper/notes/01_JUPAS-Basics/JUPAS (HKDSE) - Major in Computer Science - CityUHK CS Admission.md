## Answer

## JUPAS (HKDSE) - Major in Computer Science

### I am going to take the HKDSE. How can I join the Major Computer Science?

- If you want to join our major, you need to apply for JS1204 BSc Computer Science in your JUPAS application.
- For the details and entrance requirements of JS1204, you may click [here](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/programmes/bsc-computer-science)
