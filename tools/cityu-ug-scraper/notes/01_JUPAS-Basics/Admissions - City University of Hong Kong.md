## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Admissions | City University of Hong Kong**?

## Answer

Located in the heart of Hong Kong, City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK) has a well-earned reputation as an innovative hub for research and professional education and for addressing global issues and empowering positive change.

## Did You Know?

CityUHK take significant strides forward in consolidating and building upon themes set forth in our Strategic Plan 2015–2020, such as discovery and innovation, interdisciplinarity, global partnerships, knowledge transfer, and world-class governance and branding.

## Undergraduate Admissions

We welcome applications to CityUHK through the International Admissions Scheme/Non-JUPAS Admissions Scheme if you are applying on the strength of qualifications other than Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) or mainland China’s National University Entrance Examination (NUEE/Gaokao), regardless of whether you are studying in Hong Kong or overseas.

## Postgraduate Admissions

CityUHK offers a wide range of full-time and part-time Master’s programmes, from one to three years in length. We operate a semester system and candidates' academic progress is usually assessed through a combination of written examinations and project assessments throughout the year .
