## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Bachelor of Science in Computer Science | Department of Computer Science - IT Professional Internship - Employers**?

## Answer

## IT Professional Internship

# Information for Employers

## Procedure

1. Every year invitations to join the scheme are sent to companies around November.
2. The internship period is 9 months and it starts in summer, earliest in July and latest in September.
3. Students work in internship 4 days/week, with Fridays off to attend classes at CityUHK.
4. Employers will interview students prior to confirmation of internship, and the company will assign a working supervisor for the students afterwards.
5. The performance of the students will be jointly assessed by both academic and working supervisors.

## Timeline

### For NEW Internship Company:

| Event | Action By | Date | 
|---|---|---|
| Invitation Letter to Internship Companies | CityUHK CS Department | November / December | 
| Companies request internship students by: **[Create job post](https://pmsext.cs.cityu.edu.hk/job/)**; or **[Contact Us](/academic-programmes/bsc-computer-science/contact-us)** directly | Internship Company | December / January | 
| Interview Arrangement | CityUHK CS Department Internship Company | March - May | 
| Employment Confirmation | Internship Company | May - June | 

### 

For EXISTING Internship Company:

| Event | Action By | Date | 
|---|---|---|
| Student Performance Review (Interim) | Internship Company | Start of December | 
| Student Performance Review (Final) | Internship Company | April | 
| Student Assessment | CityUHK CS Department Academic Supervisor | May | 

## Roles of Internship Company

- Assign work supervisor;
- Initial meeting with academic supervisor;
- Conduct regular student performance assessment (in December & April);
- Market rate trainee/intern salary;
- Welcome to keep close contact with the academic supervisor.

## Roles of Our Department

- Assign academic supervisor;
- Initial meeting with work supervisor;
- Conduct regular student performance assessment (in December & April);
- Support internship company in student coaching & counseling.
