## Answer

## Employment Sector

### Employment Sectors and Job Nature of the Graduates of BSc Computer Science?

- **Computer Science knowledge** is essential in the whole society. Thus, a broad range of job opportunities are suitable to our **graduates**.
- As shown in the following figure, our graduates have been widely spread in various **employment sectors** including Business Service, Engineering, Architectural & Technical Services and Finance/Insurance.

The following statistics are based on the results of the Graduate Employment Survey conducted by City University of Hong Kong for BSc Computer Science graduates.

#### Main findings for Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BScCS) graduates in 2023

## Engagement Statistics

**98.9% Full-time Employment\***

\* *Ratio of the number of full-time employed graduates to the total number of the full-time/ part-time employed and unemployed*

60.8% of 2023 BScCS graduates engaged in employment, 16.3% furthered their studies and 15% returned to home country, emigrated or plan to emigrate.
