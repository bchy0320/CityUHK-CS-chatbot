## Curriculum Overview

## For Catalogue Term of Semester A, 2022/2023 and thereafter

A student can apply for the Minor in Computing award provided that the student satisfies the following :

- having acquired 15 credits of CS courses with an average GPA of 2 or higher; and
- of the courses taken that constitute the 15 credits in 1 above, the following constraints should be observed :

Note on Mutually Exclusive: A course, such as CS1102 is exclusive with CS1302, meaning that when students choose CS1102, they cannot use CS1302 to fulfill the Minor in Computing requirements.

Minimum CGPA requirements for declaring the Minor: To be eligible, applicants must have achieved a CGPA of 3.0 or above, and should seek advance approval from their home department.

In addition, the following requirements are applied:

Students who study the following Majors cannot take the Minor in Computing:

- Computer Science
- Cybersecurity
- Computer Science and Computational Finance and Financial Technology
- Computer and Data Engineering
- Creative Media
- Information Engineering

A student who intends to take the Minor in Computing should seek approval from his/her home department and the Department of Computer Science.
