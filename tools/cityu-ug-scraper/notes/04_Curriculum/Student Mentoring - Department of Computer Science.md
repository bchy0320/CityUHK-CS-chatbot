## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Student Mentoring | Department of Computer Science**?

## Answer

## Professional Mentorship Programme

### Objectives

- To provide a platform for students to interact with senior IT professionals in Hong Kong.
- To gain exposure to different areas in IT, e.g. software development, support, networking, project management, etc., as well as obtain guidance to shape students' career paths.
- To provide an opportunity for students to develop communication and soft skills.

### Benefits for Students

- Obtain guidance for future career development.
- Understand IT working environment.
- Extend the social network.

### Duration, Activities and Period

- The programme operates for a duration of 10 months.
- Regular Meetings between mentors and mentees can be in any format ranging from touring around mentors' companies, sharing, happy hours, to attending professional activities, etc..
- Tentative schedule: October 2025 - July 2026

### Second Gathering

Building on established connections, the Department of Computer Science successfully held the second cohort meeting of the Professional Mentorship Programme on 25 March 2026. The event saw 46 mentors, mentees, and graduates reconvene to dive deeper into industry realities. Representatives from premier organizations—such as Microsoft, Meta, PwC, HKBN Enterprise Solutions, and PCCW—shared targeted insights into navigating the modern tech landscape, helping students map out their career trajectories and strengthen vital industry ties.

The Department of Computer Science hosted the first gathering of the Professional Mentorship Programme on 6 November 2025, bringing together 63 participants, including mentors, mentees and BSc graduates. Mentors from leading organisations such as TCL, Meta, SmarTone, Siemens, PCCW, Biel Crystal, and AI Guided joined the event to share experiences and engage with students. The event provided opportunities to gain career insights and build meaningful professional connections.
