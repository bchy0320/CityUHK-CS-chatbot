## Answer

## Study Streams

### What are Study Streams ?

- **FIVE Study Streams** are provided to allow students to study in depth in a **Selected area** in **Computer Science** based on their own interests and future career aspiration.
- The **Study Streams** also help to enhance the students' competitiveness in the job market, and further studies and research.
- Each **Stream** consists of **9 credits** of stream specific courses.
