## Final Year Project Showcase

### Final Year Project Exhibition 2026

To showcase the outstanding creativity, innovation, and research achievements of the graduating cohort, the 2026 edition of the Final Year Project (FYP) Exhibition was successfully held on 22 April 2026. Featuring a vibrant display of projects spanning multiple disciplines within the Computer Science realm, the event allowed academic staff to engage directly with students and explore thought-provoking ideas at the forefront of the field.

A major highlight of the exhibition was the Best Poster Awards, which celebrated excellence in both research content and visual communication. Evaluated by our academic staff for clarity, originality, and impact, the awards set a high standard for future participants.
