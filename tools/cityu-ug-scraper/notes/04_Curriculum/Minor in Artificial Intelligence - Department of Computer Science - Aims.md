## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Minor in Artificial Intelligence | Department of Computer Science - Aims**?

## Answer

## Aims

In view of the transformative impacts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) across a wide range of sectors including education, finance, healthcare, and transportation, there is an increasing demand for professionals with expertise in AI technologies. The Minor in Artificial Intelligence is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of fundamental AI principles and practical skills. The minor programme covers essential topics such as deep learning, machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision, combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience. The completion of this programme will enable students to apply their knowledge of AI technologies to address real-world challenges effectively in various disciplines. By aligning with the Hong Kong Government's initiatives to promote innovation and technology as key drivers of economic growth, the minor programme supports the objective of educating the next generation of skilled professionals capable of driving future advancements in technology.


### Aims and Objectives

The Minor in Artificial Intelligence aims to provide students with comprehensive education in the understanding and application of AI technologies and methodologies. After completing the Minor in Artificial Intelligence, a student should be able to:

- Master the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies
- Utilise AI techniques to solve complex problems
- Develop AI-driven applications and systems
- Keep abreast of the current trends and issues in AI
