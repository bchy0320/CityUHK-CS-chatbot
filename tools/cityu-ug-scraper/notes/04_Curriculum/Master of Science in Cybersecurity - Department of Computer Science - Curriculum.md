## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Master of Science in Cybersecurity | Department of Computer Science - Curriculum**?

## Answer

## Programme Structure

Courses in the programme are categorised into Core Courses and Elective Courses. To obtain the award of Master of Science in Cybersecurity, students are required to take

- All 10 credit units of the core courses, AND
- At least 21 credit units of the Elective Courses.

Students are required to take at least 12 credit units of the elective courses in Group I and at most 9 credit units of the elective courses in Group II to complete the programme.

Refer to [Taught Postgraduate Catalogue](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/catalogue/pg/202627/catalogue/catalogue_TP.htm?page=TP/TP_course_CS.htm) for more information of individual courses.

More information:

- All the Project and Internship courses are mutually exclusive.
- Students must fulfill the pre-requisite requirements before taking the Project or Internship courses.
- Students are responsible for finding a suitable internship, if a student cannot find suitable internship, the student may take other taught course to fulfill the programme requirements.
- Students are required to take at least 12 credit units of the elective courses in Group I and at most 9 credit units of the elective courses in Group II to complete the programme.

## 2-YEAR PLAN 2
* **Year 1:**
  * 4 CORE COURSES = 10 CREDITS
  * + 2 ELECTIVE COURSES = 6 CREDITS
* **Year 2:**
  * + 1 PROJECT COURSE (SEM B & SUMMER TERM) = 6 CREDITS
  * + 3 ELECTIVE COURSES = 9 CREDITS
* **Total:**
  * = 10 COURSES = 31 CREDITS

**Relationships:**
Both plans share the same Year 1 structure (4 Core + 2 Elective) and result in the same total number of credits (31). They differ in Year 2 options: Plan 1 uses 5 Electives, while Plan 2 substitutes a Project Course and 3 Electives, resulting in one fewer total course.
