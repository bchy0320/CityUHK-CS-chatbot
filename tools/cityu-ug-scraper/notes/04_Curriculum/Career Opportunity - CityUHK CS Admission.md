## Answer

## After Graduation At-A-Glance

### What Career Opportunities are available to Computer Science graduates?

- Most Graduates work as IT professionals (in various job natures) in the IT or IT-related industries.
- Some Graduates choose the academic career path and proceed to further studies for master or PhD degrees.
- Some Graduates may decide to work in the industries that may **NOT** be directly related to IT.
- Many of them are very successful because the training at CityU has equipped them with strong problem-solving skills and logical thinking.
