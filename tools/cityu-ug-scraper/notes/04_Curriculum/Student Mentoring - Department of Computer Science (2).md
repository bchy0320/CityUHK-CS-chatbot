## Student Mentoring Scheme

The Department's Student Mentoring Scheme helps new students to adapt smoothly to university life and shapes senior students as big brothers and sisters for our freshmen. Throughout the academic year, regular informal gatherings are organized by student mentors to help new students adapt smoothly to university life. A variety of activities are organized such as orientation tours, company visits and study workshops in which student mentors can share their experiences with new students as well as monitor their continued progress.
