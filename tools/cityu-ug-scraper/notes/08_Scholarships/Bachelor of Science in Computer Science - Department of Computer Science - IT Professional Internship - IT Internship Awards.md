# IT Internship Awards

The Department of Computer Science thanks the following companies for their generous donations to support our IT Professional Award Scheme. Award recipients will be able to take up 9-month of internship in these prestigious companies. The support will greatly help in nurturing our young talents and developing our student and academic activities.
