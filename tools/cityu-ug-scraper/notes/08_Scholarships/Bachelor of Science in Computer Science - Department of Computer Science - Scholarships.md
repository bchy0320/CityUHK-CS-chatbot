## Scholarships

Every academic year, there are scholarship nominations for our students. The nominees should have outstanding academic performance, possess good personal qualities and achievements such as active participation in extra-curricular activities and community services.


List of scholarships awarded to our students in recent years:

- **Biu Chun Watch Hands Scholarship**
- **Chan Wing Fui Scholarships**
- **Chiap Hua Cheng's Foundation Scholarships**
- **HKSAR Government Scholarship**
- **Hong Kong Association of University Women**
- **Hong Kong Mediation and Arbitration Centre Professional Award Scheme**
- **HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship**
- **IEEE (Hong Kong Section) Prizes**
- **[InfoTech Job Market Driven Scholarship](/-/media/project/cityuhk/academic/cs/pdf/scholarships_infotech.pdf?rev=0872b62eecdd49c787bc780b79dea126&hash=5110039364FB1133C56531894CFB6455)**
