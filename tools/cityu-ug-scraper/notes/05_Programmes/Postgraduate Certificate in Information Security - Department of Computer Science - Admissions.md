## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Postgraduate Certificate in Information Security | Department of Computer Science - Admissions**?

## Answer

## Admissions

To be eligible for admission to the Postgraduate Certificate in Information Security (PGCINSE) programme, you must satisfy the University's **[General Entrance Requirements](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/pg/taught-postgraduate-programmes/entrance-requirements)**, and also have:

- a recognised bachelor's degree in a computing discipline such as Computer Studies, Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or equivalent; or
- a recognised bachelor's degree in a related discipline such as Electronic Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Manufacturing Engineering, Quantitative Analysis, or equivalent, together with applicable working experience in information technology.

*(Discontinuation of PGC in Information Security: There will be no intake starting from 2023/2024.)*
