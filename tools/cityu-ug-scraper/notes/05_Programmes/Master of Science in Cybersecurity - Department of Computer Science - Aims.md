## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Master of Science in Cybersecurity | Department of Computer Science - Aims**?

## Answer

## Aims

The programme aims to:

- Enable computer professionals to strengthen and upgrade their technical capabilities in cybersecurity,
- Enhance students' comprehensive knowledge and profound comprehension of specialised cybersecurity domains, encompassing both technical and practical aspects, and
- Prepare graduates to take up research and advanced innovative development work in the industry or pursue higher research studies in cybersecurity.

## Programme Intended Learning Outcomes (PILOs)

- Apply tools and techniques in the development of cybersecurity software and propose solutions,
- Apply cybersecurity concepts and technologies, as well as domain-specific tools and techniques, in the design of quality cybersecurity systems,
- Work effectively as member of a team in the development of cybersecurity systems,
- Delineate key issues of specific areas in cybersecurity and develop potential solutions for tackling problems in these areas.
