## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Master of Science in Computer Science | Department of Computer Science - Financial Aids**?

## Answer

## Financial Aids

## **Continuing Education Fund (for local students)**

The three courses CS5367, CS6175, and EC5001 are valid for Continuing Education Fund (CEF) reimbursement till 31 March 2023. Local students may apply for CEF reimbursement if they take these courses which commence on or before 31 March 2023. Students taking these courses which commence on or after 1 April 2023 will not be eligible for claiming CEF reimbursement.


**Applicable to CEF reimbursable courses commenced before 1 April 2019:** Eligible applicants will be reimbursed 80% of the course fee, subject to a maximum sum of HK$10,000 on successful completion of the course.


**Applicable to CEF reimbursable courses commenced on or after 1 April 2019:** Eligible applicants will be reimbursed 80% of the course fee for the 1st HK$10,000; and 60% of the course fee for the 2nd HK$10,000, subject to a maximum sum of HK$20,000 on successful completion of the course.


Please refer to the **[website](https://www.wfsfaa.gov.hk/cef/en/preparation/overview.htm)** for details regarding eligibility and the normal application procedures, or the hotline 3142 2277 for enquiries.

## **Education Loan (for local students)**

Successful applicants are eligible to apply for the Government's Non-means-tested Loan Scheme. Application forms are obtainable from each District Office and the Student Finance Office of the Hong Kong Government.


24-hour Enquiry Hotline: 2802 2345

Website: **[www.wfsfaa.gov.hk/sfo](http://www.wfsfaa.gov.hk/sfo)**
