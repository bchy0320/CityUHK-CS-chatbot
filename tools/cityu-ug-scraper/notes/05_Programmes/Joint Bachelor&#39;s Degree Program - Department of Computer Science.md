## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Joint Bachelor&#39;s Degree Program | Department of Computer Science**?

## Answer

## CityUHK-ColumbiaU Joint Bachelor's Degree Program (JBDP)

Our university has partnered with Columbia University (ColumbiaU) and established a Joint Bachelor's Degree Program (JBDP) for students under certain majors (including CS) to apply. Students enrolled in this JBDP are initially in Hong Kong to take courses at CityUHK and then will go to New York to take courses at ColumbiaU in their final 2 years. After students complete this JBDP by fulfilling the graduation requirements from CityUHK as well as ColumbiaU, they will earn 2 undergraduate degrees: one from CityUHK and another from ColumbiaU.

Students should check out the official webpage [https://cityu-hk.gs.columbia.edu](https://cityu-hk.gs.columbia.edu/) and pay attention to their emails for any latest information about this JBDP.

## To find out more

You should check out the [JBDP Canvas page](https://canvas.cityu.edu.hk/courses/47465/pages/cityu-columbiau-joint-bachelors-degree-program-jbdp) (only available for current undergraduate CS students) that contains a lot of useful information about the JDBP.

You may contact the CityUHK-ColumbiaU Joint Bachelor's Degree Program Coordinator if you cannot find the answers to your questions regarding the JBDP from the above Canvas page.
