## Major Structure

This major has a three year structure. Students acquire fundamental computer science knowledge as well as general knowledge in their first year of study. With satisfactory attainment, students will take the year-long course of IT Professional Internship (ITPP) to gain real-world like work experience. Alternatively, subject to job availability and study schedule, students may choose to work in companies joining our internship scheme. During the internship year, students will take one day off weekly from their work to complete a number of elective courses. Students will also take up a year-long project in which they work independently under the supervision of a faculty member in the last year.

## Entrance Requirements

To be eligible for admission, you must satisfy the **[General Entrance Requirements](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/admissions/non-jupas-senior-year-admission)**.

You will be considered as having met the major entrance requirements if you hold a Higher Diploma or an Associate
    Degree in a computing-related discipline, or an equivalent qualification.


For senior-year admission, applicants are normally expected to have completed the AD/HD award with a CGPA of
    >=3.0, or an equivalent overall mark or credit award.


Click **[HERE](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/admissions/non-jupas-senior-year-admission)** for more information on application.

## Entrance Scholarships and Grants

## The Department of Computer Science Special Admission Scheme Scholarships

### Objective:

The awards are restricted to students admitted into the BSc Computer Science
                                        Advanced Standing II through the Special Admission Scheme with outstanding
                                        academic performance. The minimum CGPA is 3.4 or equivalent.


### No. of Award:

There shall be two scholarships in maximum. The maximum value of each scholarship shall be HK$5,000. The amount shall be used to settle the payment of the tuition fee for the BSc Computer Science programme.

## The Department of Computer Science Entrance Grants

### Objective:

The awards shall be given to students with outstanding entrance qualifications admitted into the BSc Computer Science Advanced Standing I through direct application.

### No. of Award:

There shall be two awards in maximum. The maximum value of each award shall be HK$5,000. The amount shall be used to settle the payment of the tuition fee for the BSc Computer Science programme.
