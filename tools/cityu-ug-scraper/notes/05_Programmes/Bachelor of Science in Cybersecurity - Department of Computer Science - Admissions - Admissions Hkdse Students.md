## Admissions for HKDSE Students

# JUPAS Code: **JS1218**

## Why choose BSc Cyberscurity?

Cybersecurity is a vital and dynamic field driven by the increasing digitalization of our world. Our BSc Cybersecurity programme will equip students with the specialized knowledge and skills required to safeguard digital assets, respond to cyber threats, and mitigate the escalating risks of cybercrime. With the growing reliance on technology across all sectors, the demand for cybersecurity professionals has surged, offering an intellectually stimulating and impactful career path.

## Major Structure

This major has **a four-year structure**.

## Entrance Requirements

For admission to JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity, JUPAS HKDSE applicants must meet the following entrance requirements.

| Subject | Minimum Level Required | 
|---|---|
| English Language | Level 3 | 
| Chinese Language | Level 3 | 
| Mathematics | Level 3 | 
| Citizenship and Social Development | Attained | 
| Elective 1\* | Level 3 in one elective subject from: **Biology / Chemistry / Information and Communication Technology / M1 / M2 /                                         Physics** | 
| Elective 2\* | Level 3 in any elective subjects | 

\* Notes:

1. Unspecified elective subjects may include "other languages" at grade E or above.
2. If students take both M1 and M2, they are counted as one subject only.
3. Applied Learning subjects are not counted as elective subjects.
