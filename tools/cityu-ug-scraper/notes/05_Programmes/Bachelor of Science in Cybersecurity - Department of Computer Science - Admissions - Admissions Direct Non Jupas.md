## Why choose BSc Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is a vital and dynamic field driven by the increasing digitalization of our world. Our BSc Cybersecurity programme will equip students with the specialized knowledge and skills required to safeguard digital assets, respond to cyber threats, and mitigate the escalating risks of cybercrime. With the growing reliance on technology across all sectors, the demand for cybersecurity professionals has surged, offering an intellectually stimulating and impactful career path.

## Major Structure

This major has a three-year structure.

## Entrance Requirements

To be eligible for admission, you must satisfy the **[General Entrance Requirements](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/admissions/non-jupas-senior-year-admission)**.

You will be considered as having met the major entrance requirements if you hold a Higher Diploma or an Associate Degree in a computing-related discipline, or an equivalent qualification.

For senior-year admission, applicants are normally expected to have completed the AD/HD award with a CGPA of >=3.0, or an equivalent overall mark or credit award.

Click [HERE](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/admissions/non-jupas-senior-year-admission) for more information on application.
