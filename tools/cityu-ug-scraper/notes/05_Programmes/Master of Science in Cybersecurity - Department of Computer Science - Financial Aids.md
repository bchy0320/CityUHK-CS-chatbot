## Question

What does the official CityUHK page say about **Master of Science in Cybersecurity | Department of Computer Science - Financial Aids**?

## Answer

## Financial Aids

## Fee Reduction with Credit Unit Transfer

Based on previous study and performance, up to 9 credit units can be transferred. The fees for the transferred credits are waived.

Please refer to the **[website](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/sgs/student/masters/records/credittransfer)** for details.

## Education Loan (for local students)

Successful applicants are eligible to apply for the Government's Non-means-tested Loan Scheme. Application forms are obtainable from each District Office and the Student Finance Office of the Hong Kong Government.

Enquiries: 2150 6223

Website: [www.wfsfaa.gov.hk/sfo](http://www.wfsfaa.gov.hk/sfo)

## Other Scholarships

For other Scholarships and Prizes donated by individuals, private organizations, professional bodies, please visit the website from **[Student Development Services](http://www.cityu.edu.hk/sds)** for details.
