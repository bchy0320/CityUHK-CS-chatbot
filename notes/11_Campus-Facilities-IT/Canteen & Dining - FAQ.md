# Canteen & Dining - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK has **10 catering outlets** on campus. The main canteens are **City Express** (5/F, BOC Complex, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), **AC2 Canteen** (3/F, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), and **AC3 Bistro** (7/F, Lau Ming Wai Academic Building, western, 07:30–21:00 Mon–Sat). For Chinese food, **City Chinese Restaurant** (8/F, BOC Complex) serves dim sum. Most outlets accept **Octopus, credit card, AlipayHK, Apple Pay, and WeChat Pay HK**; some are cash-only. **AC2 Canteen** is closed on Chinese New Year; **AC3 Bistro**, **CMCAFE**, **The Street Cafe**, **5380 Cafe**, and **Coffee Cart** are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Question

What are the common questions about **canteens and dining** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: Does CityUHK have a canteen on campus?

Yes. CityUHK has **10 catering outlets** on campus, including canteens, bistros, restaurants, and cafes. The main ones are **City Express**, **AC2 Canteen**, and **AC3 Bistro**.

## Q2: Where is the main canteen?

The largest canteen is **City Express** (1000 seats) on **5/F, Bank of China (Hong Kong) Complex**. **AC2 Canteen** (860 seats) is on **3/F, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building**.

## Q3: What are the opening hours?

- **City Express**: 07:30–21:00 (Mon–Sun)
- **AC2 Canteen**: 07:30–21:00 (Mon–Sun), closed on Chinese New Year
- **AC3 Bistro**: 07:30–21:00 (Mon–Sat), closed on Sun & public holidays
- **City Chinese Restaurant**: 11:00–22:30 (Mon–Sat); 09:30–22:30 (Sun & public holidays)
- **Faculty Lounge**: 11:00–22:00 (Mon–Sun)
- **Lodge Bistro**: 07:30–21:30 (Mon–Sun)
- **CMCAFE**: 08:00–20:00 (Mon–Fri); 08:00–17:00 (Sat)
- **The Street Cafe**: 07:30–21:00 (Mon–Fri); 09:00–19:00 (Sat & Sun); 09:00–17:00 (sem break & public holidays)
- **5380 Cafe (Kebab Station)**: 10:00–20:00 (Mon–Sat)
- **Coffee Cart**: 08:00–20:00 (Mon–Fri); 08:00–17:00 (Sat)

## Q4: What payment methods are accepted?

Most outlets accept **Cash, Credit Card, Octopus Card, AlipayHK, Apple Pay, BoC Pay, PayMe, Union Pay, and WeChat Pay HK**. Some outlets are **cash-only** or accept a limited set:
- **City Chinese Restaurant**: Cash only
- **CMCAFE**: Cash only
- **The Street Cafe**: Cash only
- **5380 Cafe (Kebab Station)**: Cash, Octopus Card
- **Coffee Cart**: No payment method listed

## Q5: Is there halal food on campus?

Yes. **5380 Cafe (Kebab Station)** on **5/F, Bank of China (Hong Kong) Complex** serves **Halal** food and kebabs. It is open 10:00–20:00 (Mon–Sat), closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Q6: Is there a place to get coffee on campus?

Yes. There are several cafes and coffee outlets:
- **CMCAFE** (3/F, Run Run Shaw Creative Media Centre)
- **The Street Cafe** (3/F, Lau Ming Wai Academic Building)
- **Coffee Cart** (4/F, Yeung Kin Man Academic Building, Purple Zone)
- **5380 Cafe (Kebab Station)** (5/F, BOC Complex)

## Q7: Are there restaurants near campus?

Yes. Many restaurants are reachable within a **10-minute walk** in the nearby residential area and shopping mall, serving food of various cuisines.
