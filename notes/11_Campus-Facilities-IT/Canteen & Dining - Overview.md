# Canteen & Dining - Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK has **10 catering outlets** on campus. The main ones are **City Express** (5/F, Bank of China (Hong Kong) Complex, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), **AC2 Canteen** (3/F, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), **AC3 Bistro** (7/F, Lau Ming Wai Academic Building, western, 07:30–21:00 Mon–Sat), **City Chinese Restaurant** (8/F, BOC Complex, dim sum, 11:00–22:30 Mon–Sat), and **Faculty Lounge** (9/F, BOC Complex, western, 11:00–22:00 daily). There are also **Lodge Bistro**, **CMCAFE**, **The Street Cafe**, **5380 Cafe (Kebab Station)**, and a **Coffee Cart**. Most accept **Octopus, credit card, AlipayHK, Apple Pay, and WeChat Pay HK**; some are cash-only.

## Payment Methods

Most outlets accept a wide range of payment methods, including **Cash, Credit Card, Octopus Card, AlipayHK, Apple Pay, BoC Pay, PayMe, Union Pay, and WeChat Pay HK**. Some outlets (e.g. **City Chinese Restaurant**, **CMCAFE**, **The Street Cafe**, **5380 Cafe**, **Coffee Cart**) are **cash-only** or accept a limited set of methods.

## Notes

- **AC2 Canteen** is closed on **Chinese New Year**.
- **AC3 Bistro** is closed on **Sundays and public holidays**.
- **CMCAFE**, **The Street Cafe**, **5380 Cafe**, and **Coffee Cart** are closed on **Sundays and public holidays**.
- **The Street Cafe** has reduced hours (09:00–17:00) during **semester breaks and public holidays**.
- Many restaurants are also reachable within a **10-minute walk** in the nearby residential area and shopping mall.
