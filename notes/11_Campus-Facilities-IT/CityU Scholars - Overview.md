## Question

What is **CityU Scholars** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## What is CityU Scholars?

**CityU Scholars** is an integrated research intelligence system that aggregates CityUHK's research information from numerous internal and external sources. It ensures the data that drives strategic decisions is trusted, comprehensive and accessible in a central repository, providing a single source of truth on a timely basis.

## Key Features

- **Searchable web portal** - Showcases CityUHK's researchers to the public with their research output, patents, projects, research activities, prize/honours, student theses, datasets, and impacts
- **External system integration** - Highly integrated with external systems including Elsevier's Scopus®, Altmetric, handle.net and more, forming a comprehensive research portal
- **Research portfolio management** - All CityUHK staff and research students can log in to manage their research portfolio

## Who Can Use It

- **All CityUHK staff** can log in to manage their research portfolio
- **Research students** can log in to manage their research portfolio

## How to Access

- Log in with your **CityUHK EID**
- The Library provides support for CityUHK Scholars (contact lbscholars@cityu.edu.hk or 3442-6935)
