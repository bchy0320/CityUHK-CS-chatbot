# Health Centre - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> The **Young Chung-Yee Health Centre** is CityUHK's on-campus medical and dental clinic, located on **4/F, Bank of China (Hong Kong) Complex** (near AC1 Canteen / City Express). The **Medical Clinic** is open **Mon–Fri 9:00am–1:00pm and 1:30pm–6:00pm** (lunch break 1:00–1:30pm, emergency only; registration closes 5:45pm) and **Sat 9:00am–12:15pm** (registration closes 12:00pm); closed Sun and public holidays. The **Dental Clinic** is open **Mon–Fri 9:00am–1:00pm and 2:00pm–6:00pm** and **Sat 9:00am–12:15pm**; closed Sun and public holidays. **Medical hotline: 3442-6066**, **Dental hotline: 3442-6052**.
