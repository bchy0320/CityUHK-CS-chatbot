# Campus Facilities - Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK's campus offers a wide range of facilities for students. The **Run Run Shaw Library** (Level 2–3, Yeung Kin Man Academic Building) holds over 1 million print books and about 2 million e-books. **Computers and Internet** are available via Express Terminals and the Computing Services Centre (CSC), with Wi-Fi across most of campus. The **Chan Feng Men-ling Chan Shuk-lin Language Centre** provides language training and self-access study. **Activity Venues** include study rooms, discussion rooms, case rooms, studios, music rooms, and more. **Sports & Recreation Facilities** include a swimming pool, physical fitness room, table tennis room, and outdoor basketball court, plus the **Joint Sports Centre** at Renfrew Road (soccer field, running track, tennis courts). The **Young Chung-Yee Health Centre** provides medical and dental care. **Canteen & Dining** includes 10 catering outlets such as City Express, AC2 Canteen, and AC3 Bistro. Online services include **AIMS**, **CityUHK Portal / Canvas**, and the **CityUHK Mobile** app.

## Question

What **facilities** does CityUHK have on campus?

## Answer

CityUHK's campus offers the following facilities:

- **Run Run Shaw Library** — Level 2–3, Yeung Kin Man Academic Building
- **Computers and Internet** — Express Terminals, Computing Services Centre (CSC), Wi-Fi
- **Chan Feng Men-ling Chan Shuk-lin Language Centre** — language training and self-access study
- **Activity Venues** — study rooms, discussion rooms, case rooms, studios, music rooms, and more
- **Sports & Recreation Facilities** — swimming pool, physical fitness room, table tennis room, outdoor basketball court
- **Joint Sports Centre** — Renfrew Road, soccer field, running track, tennis courts
- **Health Care** — Young Chung-Yee Health Centre (medical and dental)
- **Canteen & Dining** — 10 catering outlets including City Express, AC2 Canteen, AC3 Bistro, City Chinese Restaurant
- **AIMS** — course registration, class schedule, examination timetable and results
- **CityUHK Portal / Canvas** — online services and campus facility booking
- **CityUHK Mobile** — official app for staff, students and visitors

### Run Run Shaw Library

Located on **Level 2 (Yellow and Red Zones) and Level 3 of the Yeung Kin Man Academic Building**, the Library occupies a central location. Its collection includes **more than 1 million print books** and **about 2 million electronic books**.

### Computers and Internet

**Express Terminals** in the Yeung Kin Man Academic Building and computers in the **Computing Services Centre (CSC)** are available for students' use. **Wireless LAN** is available in most parts of the campus, including the ground area of the Student Residence.

### Chan Feng Men-ling Chan Shuk-lin Language Centre (LC)

The Centre delivers high-quality, practical **language training** and provides facilities and guidance for independent language study. It promotes and supports informal activities for language practice.

### Activity Venues

Many kinds of activity venues are available on campus, including **study rooms, discussion rooms, case rooms, studios, dancing rooms, dark rooms, music rooms, band rooms, and tearooms**.

### Sports & Recreation Facilities

Sports facilities available for booking include the **physical fitness room** and **table tennis room**. For outdoor sports, you may use the on-campus **swimming pool** and the **outdoor basketball court** located opposite the student residence halls.

### Joint Sports Centre

The **Joint Sports Centre** at Renfrew Road, Kowloon Tong provides an **11-a-side standard soccer field**, an **all-weather running track**, a **multi-purpose court** for basketball, volleyball and handball, **tennis courts**, and other facilities.

### Health Care

The **Young Chung-Yee Health Centre** is a medical and dental clinic on campus that provides general medical consultations and dental care services to members of CityUHK.

### Canteen & Dining

CityUHK has **10 catering outlets** on campus. The main ones are **City Express** (5/F, Bank of China (Hong Kong) Complex, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), **AC2 Canteen** (3/F, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building, fast food, 07:30–21:00 daily), and **AC3 Bistro** (7/F, Lau Ming Wai Academic Building, western, 07:30–21:00 Mon–Sat). **City Chinese Restaurant** (8/F, BOC Complex) serves dim sum. Most outlets accept **Octopus, credit card, AlipayHK, Apple Pay, and WeChat Pay HK**; some are cash-only.

### Administrative Information Management System (AIMS)

Through **AIMS**, students can manage or view their course registration, class schedule, examination timetable and results, Student Residence application, personal information, student financial account, campus job application, and application for certain student services.

### CityUHK Portal / Canvas

An electronic infrastructure provides various online services and information related to study and life at CityUHK, including news about activities and academic matters. Campus facilities can be booked through the e-Portal.

### CityUHK Mobile

An official app of City University of Hong Kong providing a convenient platform for staff, students and visitors to access important information about CityUHK.
