# Library Opening Hours - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> The **Run Run Shaw Library** is open **Mon–Sun 8:30am–11:00pm** (9 Aug 2026 – 10 Jan 2027), closed on **25 December 2026** and **1 January 2027**. During **Semester A (31 Aug – 20 Dec 2026)**, the **Circulation Counter** is open **Mon–Fri 8:30am–9:00pm**, **Sat 9:00am–6:00pm**, **Sun 1:00pm–6:00pm**, closed on public holidays; the **IT Help Desk** is open **Mon–Fri 9:00am–5:00pm**, closed Sat/Sun. During the **Semester Break (21 Dec 2026 – 10 Jan 2027)**, the Circulation Counter is open **Mon–Fri 8:30am–6:00pm**, **Sat 9:00am–5:00pm**, closed Sun & public holidays.

## Question

What are the **opening hours** of the **CityUHK Library** (Run Run Shaw Library)?

## Answer

## Library Opening Hours

**9 August 2026 – 10 January 2027**

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Mon – Sun | 8:30am – 11:00pm |

The Library will be **CLOSED** on:
- 25 December 2026 (Christmas Day)
- 1 January 2027 (First Day of January)
