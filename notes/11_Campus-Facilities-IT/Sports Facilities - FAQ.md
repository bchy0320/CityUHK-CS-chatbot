# Sports Facilities - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK sports facilities are booked online via the **Sports Facilities Booking & Information (SFBI) System** (log in with your **EID** on Single Sign-On). You can reserve **one session per day per facility up to 7 days in advance**, and pay immediately via **CityUHK Pay**. Eligible users are **full-time staff, full/part-time students, and CityUHK Sports Complex User Card holders**. Facilities include the **Physical Fitness Room**, **Outdoor Basketball Court**, **Squash Court**, **Table Tennis Court**, **Practice Gymnasiums**, **Badminton**, **Pickleball**, **Indoor Basketball/Volleyball**, and the **Swimming Pool**. The **Joint Sports Centre** at Renfrew Road offers a soccer field, running track, multi-purpose court, and tennis courts. Web booking is available daily **08:00–23:59** (Wed until 22:59), closed on public holidays.

## Question

How do I **book a CityUHK sports facility** (sports hall, gym, court, swimming pool)?

## Answer

## How do I book a sports facility?

Use the **Sports Facilities Booking & Information (SFBI) System**. Log in with your **EID and password** on the **Single Sign-On (SSO)** function after clicking "Log in" on the SFBI logon page.

## What can I book online?

The following facilities at the **Hu Fa Kuang Sports Centre** are available for online booking by CityUHK full-time staff, full/part-time students, and CityUHK Sports Complex User Card holders:

- Badminton
- Pickleball
- Indoor Basketball
- Indoor Volleyball
- Practice Gymnasium 2, 3, 4
- Squash

## What are the booking rules?

- Reserve **one session per day** of each sports facility **up to 7 days in advance**.
- All bookings must be **confirmed and paid immediately** via **CityUHK Pay**.
- You receive an email notification from CityUHK Pay and a confirmation email from the SFBI System. The SFBI confirmation email is the **only confirmed booking** of that session.

## What are the SFBI web booking functions?

- **Home**: Make a new booking (one session per day per facility, up to 7 days in advance).
- **My Booking**: View a detailed summary of all your bookings with filters.
- **Profile**: Set up 3 most preferred facilities for a maximum of 3 days within the week for quick access.
- **My Guest Ticket**: Purchase up to four on-the-day guest tickets (log in, click "My Guest Ticket", "Purchase Guest Ticket", fill in info, pay via CityUHK Pay, receive a dynamic QR code by email).

## What are the cancellation rules?

- **Early Release**: Release a booking at least **two calendar days** (no public holidays in between) before the session. You can alter an early-released booking for another timeslot within **21 days**; if not altered within 21 days, it is automatically forfeited with no refund.
- **Late Cancellation**: Cancel after the early-release period by forfeiting the booking and fee.
- **No-Show**: Failing to use a booked facility within **15 minutes** after the session start time.
- **Penalty**: Accumulating **3 Late Cancellations or No-Shows within 30 calendar days** results in a penalty period (14 days) where online booking privileges are suspended; bookings can then be made in person at the Sports Centre Service Counters.

## When is the web booking available?

The web booking function is available every day from **08:00–23:59**, except **Wednesday from 08:00–22:59**, and is **closed on public holidays**.

## What is the Joint Sports Centre?

The **Joint Sports Centre** at Renfrew Road, Kowloon Tong provides an **11-a-side standard soccer field**, an **all-weather running track**, a **multi-purpose court** for basketball, volleyball and handball, **tennis courts**, and other facilities.
