# IT Support - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> The **IT Service Desk** is on the **4th floor of Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building (LI)**, open **Mon–Fri 08:30–21:30** and **Sat 09:00–17:00** (closed Sun/public holidays). It supports online teaching, login/password issues, Wi-Fi/VPN troubleshooting, teaching studios/classrooms, and fast-printing/scanning. Contact **3442-8340** (staff) / **3442-6488** (students) or **IT.ServiceDesk@cityu.edu.hk**; make requests or report issues via the Service Portal. Students admitted in 2026+ reset passwords via Okta Verify, while 2025 or earlier use AIMS. The **CityUHK EID** is the unique ID for accessing IT services.

## Question

What are the common questions about **IT support** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: Where is the IT Service Desk?

The **IT Service Desk** is located on the **4th floor of Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building (LI)**.

## Q2: What services does the IT Service Desk provide?

- Providing IT support for staff and students
- Support for **Online Teaching**
- Resolving **login and password** related issues
- Troubleshooting **Wi-Fi and VPN** connection problems
- Using facilities in **Teaching Studios and Classrooms**
- **Fast-printing and scanning** services

## Q3: What are the opening hours?

| Day | IT Service Desk & Classroom Support |
|-----|-------------------------------------|
| Mon - Fri | 08:30 - 21:30 |
| Sat | 09:00 - 17:00 |
| Sun | Closed |
| Public Holidays | Closed |

## Q4: How do I contact the IT Service Desk?

- **IT Service Desk**: 3442-8340 (staff) / 3442-6488 (students)
- **Email**: IT.ServiceDesk@cityu.edu.hk

## Q5: How do I make a request or report an issue?

You can **Make a Request** or **Report an Issue** via the Service Portal at https://service.cityu.edu.hk/.

## Q6: Can I get on-site support?

For staff whose problems cannot be resolved through telephone consultation, technical support staff will be dispatched to help on-site. Students may also seek help at the Service Desk.

## Q7: How do I change or reset my password?

- **Students** (admitted in 2026 intake or later): Self-service with Okta Verify push notification at https://auth.cityu.edu.hk/signin/forgot-password
- **Students** (admitted in 2025 or before): Self-service through AIMS at https://banweb.cityu.edu.hk/pls/PROD/twgkpwre_cityu.P_ResetPwd

## Q8: What is the CityUHK EID?

The **CityUHK Electronic ID (CityUHK EID)** is a unique ID automatically assigned to all staff and students for accessing IT services such as AIMS, Canvas, CAP, Email, Portal, CityUHK Scholars and CityUHKWiki.
