# Campus Location - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK is located at **Tat Chee Avenue, Kowloon Tong, Kowloon, Hong Kong**. The campus is in **Kowloon Tong**, next to the Kowloon Tong MTR station (East Rail Line and Kwun Tong Line). General enquiries: **+852 3442-7654**; 24-hour emergency: **+852 3442-8888**. See the [campus map](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/about/campus/map) for directions.

## Question

Where is the **CityUHK campus** located?

## Answer

## Address

**City University of Hong Kong**
**Tat Chee Avenue, Kowloon Tong, Kowloon, Hong Kong**

## Getting There

The campus is in **Kowloon Tong**, a major transport hub in Kowloon:

- **MTR**: Kowloon Tong Station (East Rail Line and Kwun Tong Line) is adjacent to the campus.
- **Bus / Minibus**: Numerous bus routes serve the Kowloon Tong area.
