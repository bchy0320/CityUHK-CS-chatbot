# CityU Scholars - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> **CityU Scholars** is an integrated research intelligence system aggregating CityUHK's research into a central repository. All **CityUHK staff** and **research students** can log in with their **CityUHK EID** to manage their research portfolio. The portal showcases research output, patents, projects, activities, prizes, theses, datasets, and impacts, and integrates with **Scopus®**, **Altmetric**, and **handle.net**. For help, contact **lbscholars@cityu.edu.hk** or call **3442-6935**.

## Question

What are the common questions about **CityU Scholars** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: What is CityU Scholars?

**CityU Scholars** is an integrated research intelligence system that aggregates CityUHK's research information from numerous internal and external sources into a central repository, providing a single source of truth.

## Q2: Who can use CityU Scholars?

All **CityUHK staff** and **research students** can log in to CityU Scholars to manage their research portfolio.

## Q3: What does the CityU Scholars portal showcase?

The searchable web portal showcases CityUHK's researchers to the public with their:
- Research output
- Patents
- Projects
- Research activities
- Prize/honours
- Student theses
- Datasets
- Impacts

## Q4: What external systems does CityU Scholars integrate with?

CityU Scholars is highly integrated with external systems including:
- Elsevier's **Scopus®**
- **Altmetric**
- **handle.net**
- And more

## Q5: How do I log in to CityU Scholars?

Log in with your **CityUHK EID**.

## Q6: How do I get help with CityU Scholars?

The Library provides support for CityU Scholars. Contact the CityU Scholars support team at **lbscholars@cityu.edu.hk** or call **3442-6935**.
