# Canvas System - Login & Access

> [!tip] Short answer
> Go to **canvas.cityu.edu.hk** and log in with your **CityUHK EID** and password. After logging in, teachers see the courses they teach and students see the courses they registered for. The Canvas link is also under "Quick Links" on the CityUHK homepage.

## Question

How do I **log in to Canvas** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Login to Canvas

- URL: https://canvas.cityu.edu.hk/
- Log in with your **CityUHK EID** and password

## What You See After Logging In

- **Teachers** will see the courses they are teaching
- **Students** will see what courses they have registered for

## Finding the Canvas Link

- Teachers and students can find the link to Canvas on the **CityUHK homepage** under "Quick Links"

## What is CityUHK EID?

**CityUHK Electronic ID (CityUHK EID)** is a unique ID automatically assigned to all staff and students for accessing IT services provided by the University, including Canvas, AIMS, Email, Portal, and more.
