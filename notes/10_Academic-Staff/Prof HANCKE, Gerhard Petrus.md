# Prof HANCKE, Gerhard Petrus

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof HANCKE, Gerhard Petrus** is a **Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng and MEng from Pretoria, a PhD from Cambridge, and an LLB from UNISA, and is a Fellow of IEEE and a Chartered Engineer. His research interests include **system security**, **resilient cyber-physical systems/sensing**, and **industrial Internet-of-Things**.
