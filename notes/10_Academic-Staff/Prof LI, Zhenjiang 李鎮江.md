# Prof LI, Zhenjiang 李鎮江

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof LI, Zhenjiang 李鎮江** is a **Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng from XJTU and an MPhil and PhD from HKUST, and is a Member of ACM and IEEE. His research interests include **edge and embedded AI systems**, **large language model (LLM) systems**, **artificial Internet of Things (AIoT) and smart sensing**, and **low-power and battery-free IoT systems**.
