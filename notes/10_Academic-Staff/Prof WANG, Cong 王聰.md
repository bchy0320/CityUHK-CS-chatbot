# Prof WANG, Cong 王聰

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof WANG, Cong 王聰** is the **Head & Chair Professor of Cyber Security** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng and MEng from Wuhan and a PhD from Illinois Institute of Technology, and is a Member of ACM and Fellow of IEEE. His research interests include **cloud computing**, **data and computation outsourcing security**, **access control**, and **computer and mobile networks**.
