# Prof FANG, Yuguang Michael 方玉光

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof FANG, Yuguang Michael 方玉光** is a **Global STEM Scholar & Chair Professor of Internet of Things** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds an MS from Qufu Normal U and PhDs from Case Western Reserve U and Boston U, and is a Fellow of AAAS, ACM, and IEEE. His research interests include **wireless networks**, **mobile/edge computing**, **security and privacy**, **wireless AI/ML/federated learning**, **IoT and cyber-physical systems**, **5G/6G**, **connected and autonomous driving**, and **smart health and smart grid**.
