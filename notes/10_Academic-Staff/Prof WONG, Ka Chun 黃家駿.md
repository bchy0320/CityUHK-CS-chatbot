# Prof WONG, Ka Chun 黃家駿

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof WONG, Ka Chun 黃家駿** is an **Associate Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng and MPhil from CUHK and a PhD from Toronto, and is a Fellow of APNNS.
