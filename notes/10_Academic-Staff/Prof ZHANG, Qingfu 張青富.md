# Prof ZHANG, Qingfu 張青富

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof ZHANG, Qingfu 張青富** is a **Chair Professor of Computational Intelligence** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BSc from Shanxi and an MSc and PhD from Xidian, and is a Fellow of IEEE. His research interests include **evolutionary computation**, **multiobjective optimization**, **computational intelligence**, **modern heuristics**, and **machine learning**.
