# Prof ZHU, Kening 朱克宁

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof ZHU, Kening 朱克宁** is an **Associate Professor (Home: Creative Media)** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BSc from HUST and a PhD from NUS. His research interests include **multimodal human-computer interaction**, **mobile gesture interaction**, **rapid prototyping**, **tangible/wearable user interfaces**, **virtual/augmented/mixed reality**, and **interaction design for children**.
