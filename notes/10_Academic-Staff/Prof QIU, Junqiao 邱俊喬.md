# Prof QIU, Junqiao 邱俊喬

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof QIU, Junqiao 邱俊喬** is an **Assistant Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BS from Sun Yat-sen University and a PhD from the University of California, Riverside. His research interests include **programming systems and runtime support for parallel computing**, **parallelization for data-intensive applications**, and **programming models for irregular applications**.
