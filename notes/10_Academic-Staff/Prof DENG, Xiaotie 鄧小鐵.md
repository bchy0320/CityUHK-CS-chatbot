# Prof DENG, Xiaotie 鄧小鐵

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof DENG, Xiaotie 鄧小鐵** is a **Chair Professor of Multi-Agent Economics** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BSc from Tsinghua, an MSc from Academia Sinica, and a PhD from Stanford, and is a Fellow of IEEE, ACM, SIAM, and a Member of Academia Europaea. His research interests include **AI-driven algorithmic game theory**, **LLM-based multiagent systems**, **verifiable mechanism design**, **digital and decentralized markets**, **blockchain and FinTech**, and **AI-enabled transformation of the real economy**.
