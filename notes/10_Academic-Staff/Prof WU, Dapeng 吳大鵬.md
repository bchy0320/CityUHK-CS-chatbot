# Prof WU, Dapeng 吳大鵬

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof WU, Dapeng 吳大鵬** is the **Yeung Kin Man Chair Professor in Network Science** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BE from HUST, an ME from BUPT, and a PhD from CMU, and is a Fellow of IEEE. His research interests include **artificial intelligence**, **data science**, **signal processing**, **communication**, **network science**, and **biomedical engineering**.
