# Prof MA, Wei-Ying 馬維英

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof MA, Wei-Ying 馬維英** is a **Chair Professor of Artificial Intelligence for Science** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. She holds a BS from NTHU and an MS and PhD from UC Santa Barbara, and is a Fellow of ACM and IEEE. Her research interests include **artificial intelligence**, **generative AI for biology and life sciences**, **machine learning**, **natural language processing**, and **search and data mining**.
