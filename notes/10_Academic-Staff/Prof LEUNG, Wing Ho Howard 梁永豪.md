# Prof LEUNG, Wing Ho Howard 梁永豪

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof LEUNG, Wing Ho Howard 梁永豪** is an **Associate Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng from McGill and an MSc and PhD from Carnegie Mellon. His research interests include **pattern recognition**, **multimedia and image processing**, **intelligent tools for Chinese handwriting education**, **3D human motion analysis and retrieval**, and **web-based learning technologies**.
