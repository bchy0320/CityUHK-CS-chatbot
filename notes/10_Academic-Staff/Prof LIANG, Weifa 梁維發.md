# Prof LIANG, Weifa 梁維發

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof LIANG, Weifa 梁維發** is a **Professor** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BSci from Wuhan U, an MEng from USTC, and a PhD from ANU. His research interests include **wireless sensor networks and IoT**, **mobile edge computing (MEC) and cloud computing**, **software defined networking (SDN) and network function virtualization (NFV)**, **approximation and online algorithms**, and **graph theory**.
