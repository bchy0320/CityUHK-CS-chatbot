# Prof WANG, Jianping 汪建平

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof WANG, Jianping 汪建平** is a **Chair Professor of Smart Mobility** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BSc and MSc from Nankai U and a PhD from Texas (Dallas), and is a Fellow of IEEE and AAIA. His research interests include **autonomous driving**, **service-oriented networking**, **cloud computing**, **multicast**, and **optical networking**.
