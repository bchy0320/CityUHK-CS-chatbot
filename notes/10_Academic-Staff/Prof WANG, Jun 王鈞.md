# Prof WANG, Jun 王鈞

> [!tip] Short answer
> **Prof WANG, Jun 王鈞** is a **Chair Professor of Computational Intelligence** in the Department of Computer Science at CityUHK. He holds a BEng and MEng from DLUT and a PhD from CWRU, and is a Fellow of IEEE and IAPR and a Member of Academia Europaea. His research interests include **computational intelligence**, **neural computation**, **optimization methods**, **data processing**, and **intelligent control**.
