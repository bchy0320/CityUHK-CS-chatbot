# Study Tour - FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> A **study tour** is a short-term (days to weeks) programme giving students access to organisations worldwide, developing leadership, communication, interviewing, presentation, and cultural awareness. Tours are **credit or non-credit bearing** and open to **full-time undergraduates** (some with year/GPA requirements). Find them via the **GEO website**, **college/department website**, **student email**, and **Canvas**; apply **online** (some require a written application or interview). Tours are typically held during **summer breaks**. Prepare a **passport/visa**, **travel insurance**, appropriate clothing, vaccinations, and attend the **pre-departure briefing**.

## Question

What are the common questions about **study tours** at CityUHK?

## Answer

## Q1: What is a study tour?

A study tour is a **short-term** programme (typically days to weeks) that grants students access to organisations worldwide. It allows students to engage in professional business environments while practicing and acquiring valuable skills that can benefit future careers.

## Q2: Are study tours credit-bearing?

Study tours can be **credit or non-credit bearing**. Some study tours offer academic credits, while others are for experiential learning only.

## Q3: Who can participate in study tours?

Study tours are typically open to **full-time undergraduate students**. Some study tours may have specific requirements (e.g., year of study, GPA).

## Q4: How do I find study tour opportunities?

1. Check the **GEO website** for global opportunities
2. Check your **college/department website** for specific study tour announcements
3. Look for announcements via **student email** and **Canvas**
4. Contact the **Global Engagement Office (GEO)** for more information

## Q5: How do I apply for a study tour?

Applications are typically submitted **online** via the organizing unit's platform. Some study tours may require a **written application** or **interview**. Deadlines vary by study tour.

## Q6: What skills can I develop from a study tour?

Study tours help develop:
- **Leadership**
- **Interpersonal communication**
- **Interviewing**
- **Presentation skills**
- **Cultural awareness**

## Q7: What is the difference between a study tour and exchange?

- **Study Tour**: Short-term (days to weeks), professional/business exposure, credit or non-credit bearing
- **Exchange**: Semester-long, study at a partner university, credits transferable

## Q8: Are there funding opportunities for study tours?

Some study tours may have funding support available. Students should check with the organizing unit for specific funding opportunities.

## Q9: When are study tours typically held?

Study tours are typically held during **summer breaks** and other academic breaks. The specific timing varies by study tour.

## Q10: What should I prepare for a study tour?

- **Passport** and **visa** (if required)
- **Travel insurance**
- **Appropriate clothing** for the destination
- **Any required vaccinations**
- **Pre-departure briefing** attendance
