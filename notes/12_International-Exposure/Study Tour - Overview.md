# Study Tour - Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> A **Study Tour** is a short-term (days to weeks) programme that gives students access to organisations worldwide, engaging them in professional business environments and building skills like leadership, interpersonal communication, interviewing, and presentation. Tours are **credit or non-credit bearing** and focus on business-focused cultural awareness. Unlike semester-long exchange (academic study at a partner university with transferable credits), study tours are guided visits organized by various colleges and departments, including the **GEO**, College of Business, and others.

## Question

What is a **Study Tour** at CityUHK, and how does it differ from student exchange?

## Answer

## What is a Study Tour?

Study tours grant students access to organisations worldwide, allowing them to engage in professional business environments while practicing and acquiring valuable skills that can benefit future careers.

From leadership and interpersonal communication to interviewing and presentation, these **credit and non-credit-bearing study tours** are particularly placed for business-focused cultural awareness.

## How Study Tours Differ from Exchange

| Aspect | Study Tour | Student Exchange |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Duration | Short-term (typically days to weeks) | Semester-long |
| Focus | Professional/business exposure, cultural awareness | Academic study at partner institution |
| Credits | Credit or non-credit bearing | Credits transferable back to CityUHK |
| Location | Organisations worldwide | Partner universities worldwide |
| Structure | Guided visits and activities | Full-time student at host institution |

## Who Organizes Study Tours?

Study tours are organized by various colleges and departments at CityUHK, including:

- Global Engagement Office (GEO)
- College of Business
- College of Engineering
- College of Science
- College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences
- School of Creative Media
- School of Energy and Environment
- School of Law
- Various departments (e.g., Electrical Engineering, English, Linguistics and Translation, Public and International Affairs)

## Key Facts

- **Duration**: Short-term (typically days to weeks)
- **Credit**: Credit or non-credit bearing
- **Focus**: Professional business environments, cultural awareness
- **Skills developed**: Leadership, interpersonal communication, interviewing, presentation
