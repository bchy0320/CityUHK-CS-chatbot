# CS vs IT: Key Differences

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK does not offer a separate "IT" (Information Technology) undergraduate major. The closest is **BSc Computer Science (JS1204)**, the flagship 4-year programme of the Department of Computer Science, which covers both the theoretical and practical aspects of computing. "IT" is a broader industry term; at CityUHK the computing programmes are Computer Science (JS1204), Cybersecurity (JS1218) and Data Science (JS1071).

## Question

What is the difference between a CS major and an IT major?

## Answer

CityUHK does not offer a separate **"IT" (Information Technology)** undergraduate major. The computing programmes in the College of Computing are:

- **BSc Computer Science (JS1204)** — the flagship 4-year programme, covering both the theoretical and practical aspects of computing, with five streams: Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Multimedia Computing, and Software Engineering and Project Management.
- **BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218)** — a dedicated, narrower degree where security is the through-line.
- **BSc Data Science (JS1071)** — a quantitative, data-focused programme.

"IT" is a broad industry term often used for applied, technical and support roles. At CityUHK, the closest equivalent to a general computing/IT degree is **BSc Computer Science (JS1204)**, which balances theory and practice and leads to roles such as software developer, system analyst and technology officer.

If you are comparing CS with a specific CityUHK programme, see the dedicated comparison notes (e.g. CS vs Cybersecurity). If you mean a general "IT" degree from another institution, CityUHK's CS programme is the equivalent broad computing degree here.
