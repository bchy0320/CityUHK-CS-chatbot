# Professional Mentorship Programme - Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> The **Professional Mentorship Programme** lets CS students interact with senior IT professionals in Hong Kong, gaining exposure to areas like software development, support, networking, and project management, plus career guidance and soft-skill development. It runs for **10 months** (e.g., Oct 2025 – Jul 2026 for the 2025-2026 cohort), with regular meetings in formats like company tours, sharing sessions, happy hours, and professional activities. Mentors come from leading organisations such as TCL, Meta, SmarTone, Siemens, PCCW, Biel Crystal, and AI Guided.

## Question

What is the **Professional Mentorship Programme** at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## What is the Professional Mentorship Programme?

The **Professional Mentorship Programme** provides a platform for students to interact with senior IT professionals in Hong Kong.

## Objectives

- To provide a platform for students to interact with senior IT professionals in Hong Kong
- To gain exposure to different areas in IT, e.g. software development, support, networking, project management, etc., as well as obtain guidance to shape students' career paths
- To provide an opportunity for students to develop communication and soft skills

## Benefits for Students

- Obtain guidance for future career development
- Understand IT working environment
- Extend the social network

## Duration, Activities and Period

- The programme operates for a duration of **10 months**
- Regular meetings between mentors and mentees can be in any format ranging from touring around mentors' companies, sharing, happy hours, to attending professional activities, etc.
- Tentative schedule: **October 2025 - July 2026** (for 2025-2026 cohort)

## Recent Cohorts

### 2025-2026 Cohort

- **First Gathering**: 6 November 2025, 63 participants (mentors, mentees and BSc graduates)
- **Second Gathering**: 25 March 2026, 46 participants
- Mentors from leading organisations such as TCL, Meta, SmarTone, Siemens, PCCW, Biel Crystal, and AI Guided

### 2024-2025 Cohort

- **First Gathering**: 30 October 2024, over 60 participants
- Mentors from China Mobile, Checkpoint, and Manulife

### 2023-2024 Cohort

- **First Gathering**: 9 November 2023, 49 participants
- **Second Gathering**: 27 March 2024, over 40 participants (in conjunction with the Internship Networking Event)
