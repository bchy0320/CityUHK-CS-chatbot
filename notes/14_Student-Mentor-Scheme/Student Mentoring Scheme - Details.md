## Question

What is the **Student Mentoring Scheme** at CityUHK CS?

## Answer

## What is the Student Mentoring Scheme?

The Department's **Student Mentoring Scheme** helps new students to adapt smoothly to university life and shapes senior students as "big brothers and sisters" for our freshmen.

## How does it work?

- Throughout the academic year, **regular informal gatherings** are organized by student mentors to help new students adapt smoothly to university life.
- A variety of activities are organized such as:
  - **Orientation tours**
  - **Company visits**
  - **Study workshops**
- Student mentors can share their experiences with new students as well as monitor their continued progress.

## Who can participate?

- **Mentees**: New students (freshmen) joining the Department of Computer Science
- **Mentors**: Senior students who act as "big brothers and sisters" for freshmen

## What are the benefits?

- Smooth transition to university life
- Guidance and support from senior students
- Opportunities to build friendships and networks
- Exposure to company visits and study workshops
